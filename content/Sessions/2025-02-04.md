## At [[Ivaran Sylhorn]]’s manor
- [[Jorund]] heads into another room - a grand study. There is an L shaped desk with a chair overlooking the room. Behind it is a portrait
	- [[Jorund]] gets a feeling of being watched, which seems to come from the portrait.
	- On the desk is a ceremonial crystal ball (for scrying), stacks of hastily written notes with the word “Merric” written several times and an interesting looking box.
	- [[Merric]] walks in and also feels like he is being judged. [[Merric]] feels like the portrait’s eyes snap to watch him. 
	- [[Merric]] recognises the portrait as a Drow noble, almost like the leader of a Drow house, but is clearly a man. The man is [[Ivaran Sylhorn]]. 
	- [[Ultrin]] can see the portrait has strong illusion magic and the crystal ball is magic (the box is not magic).
	- The portrait has magic that it is using to actively watch and assess [[Merric]]. To [[Ultrin]] it feels oddly like a familiar, it could possibly act as a sounding board for ideas? It has intelligence but is entirely synthetic.
	- The crystal ball has some scratches on it (shouldn’t be possible - as if its magical protections were removed) and [[Ultrin]] finds that the serial number that should be on it has been removed. 
	- [[Merric]] hides under the desk with the stack of notes (32 stealth). The notes describe the actions taken by the party for the past 24-36hours (in reverse chronological order).   
	- At the bottom of the stack (when observation started), the first recorded scry is talking with [[Jerrard|Jerrard Way]] and looking at the crates he was storing. The last note was in the sewer fighting the spider. 
	- [[Merric]] unlocks the box, it feels like it would normally be stored within a safer location. He passes is back to [[Aust]]. The box is filled with precision forgery tools - quills, ink and blank parchment (IDs, etc). There is also a seal of the black spider and seals of a number of Drow houses. [[Ultrin]] recognises the Drow houses of  Banray, Delarmgo, Oblodra, [[House T’sarran]]. The most used is the seal of the [[Black Spider]], the second most is that of [[House T’sarran]]. 
- [[Jorund]] tries to communicate with the portrait and the feeling of being watched leaves [[Merric]]. 
- [[Merric]] looks at the family tree of the [[Black Spider]] from [[Phandalin]] and it looks as though it was made up. 
- There is a cupboard off the study. It is a pristine room with 2 large square pieces of parchment on the wall 
	- a map of [[Neverwinter]] and the surrounding area with various places circled and annotated (talks about destabilisation and sewing seeds of doubt to undermine [[Lolth]] by framing her worshippers for bad things). 
	- a family tree of the [[House Vandree]] with a singular name at the top - [[Ivaran Sylhorn|Bergst Tsarran]] (of [[House T’sarran]]). The family tree has alterations and corrections and placeholders, as if the tree was retrospectively constructed. 
	- The family tree portion of Nesnar (the [[Black Spider]]) has been crossed out and the branches that beneath him have been moved, as if reinterpreting the family -  make it feel like a faked family tree.

	- In the sack is a variety of clothes - commoners, nobles, etc as if a set of disguises. A fancy set of fingerless gloves (magical) and a scarf that smells of damp/mould and also some fresh food. 
	- [[Ultrin]] grabs the gloves and sees the mirror is magic (illusion). The frame has an inscription “to reflect is to control” 
	- [[Aust]] looks in the wardrobe and finds various fancy wizard robes. 
	- [[Merric]] tries using his reflection to change something in the room.
	- [[Ultrin]] tries putting on some green robes from the wardrobe and looking in the mirror, nothing especially happens. Then he casts Light and something feels off, as if the effect happened before he finished casting the spell. It felt as if the mirror was manifesting his imagination. When he does this a 10GP pearl appears from nowhere.

	- [[Ultrin]] tries to make an expensive crystal ball with the mirror but finds it doesn’t work and finds the pearl he had earlier vanished.
	- [[Aust]] imagines that he looks like the Drow in the painting, and the front half of him (facing the mirror) suddenly looks like him. He walks into the office with the portrait, which looks at him (in a curious, respectful manner).

	- [[Merric]] checks the bookshelf - it has a combination of light reading and also poisons, rituals, the gods - these are likely from the library.

	- [[Ultrin]] feels drawn towards the wall of darkness and curious about how it works but he resists the urge (which feels familiar).

It appears to be a shield rather than harmful and feels like a powerful wizard put it in place to prevent passage.

	- He finds the barrier isn’t flush with the wood of the balcony, there is a tiny gap at the bottom that he could get through if he really wanted. There should be a power source on the other side of the barrier if he could just get there.

His misty shadow form is drawn under the darkness and he is gone.

	- The wind violently cuts across the flat roof, making it very cold but otherwise silent. The lights of [[Neverwinter]] twinkle below.
	- In the centre of the roof is a pale faced Elf, standing before an ominous dark-glow that sucks in the light around it. His skin is a brilliant white in the moonlight and there is a dark glow to his eyes, fixated on the orb.
	- There is the sound of 3 heartbeats across the roof of the manor, slow but steady. There are 3 shadowy vessels at 3 corners of the roof, drawing in the light around them in time with the heartbeat. They draw power into a shimmering barrier around the Elf. Each is protected by a corrupted elven figure, one with a mushroom cap and one spongey with pockmarks. 
	- The Elf begins chanting in a strange tongue, lifting a 3ft diameter sphere into the sky with ease.
