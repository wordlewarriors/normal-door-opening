## At [[The Farm Realm]]

-  [[Ultrin]] talks to each of the party members one by one to persuade them into joining the fight
	- He claims that we are instruments of the gods and forces of the universe 
	- [[Ultrin]] tells [[Jorund]] that he was sent by the [[Raven Queen]] to keep him safe
		- [[Jorund]] asks about [[Nand]] and is told that it was [[Nand]] that encouraged him to read the book in the first place, which was weird, and only the book is allowing him to do that now. [[Jorund]] asks about how [[Ultrin]] really knows that this is [[Nand]]. 
		- [[Ultrin]] claims that the book has never had a hold on him and has never tempted him, and he has only tricked it into believing it has. He knows (now) the book has some of his soul and that he couldn’t do the spell without the book.
		- [[Jorund]] reserves the right to kill [[Ultrin]] if the book takes over.
		- [[Jorund]] asks what success looks like - “less worms” and if we don’t succeed here, worms consume everything.
	- [[Ultrin]] tells [[Aust]] that he was sent by “that plant god, and your mum” to keep him safe.
		- [[Aust]] thinks the worms are bad. 
		- [[Aust]] decides to help as there’s nowhere else he can go and the worms are bad. 
		- [[Aust]] asks how we’ll know when the spell is complete, [[Ultrin]] isn’t sure
	- [[Ultrin]] tells [[Merric]] that he’s here because even though he chose no god, he basically chose home and even without choosing there is always someone to push you along the path
		- [[Merric]] believes that [[Ultrin]] Wants to destroy the worms.
		- [[Ultrin]] says that [[Nand]] has arranged all this, he is “the manipulator”. 
	- At [[Merric]]’s request we all go talk to [[Nand]]. He is bracing himself at the prow, looking weak and drained. [[Merric]] notices that the runes on his robe look to be weakening - he seems to be sacrificing himself for the spell. 
		- [[Jorund]] uses detect good/evil, gets evil vibes from the book. [[Nand]] doesn’t know who cast this the spell. 
		- [[Merric]] asks why [[Nand]] wanted [[Ultrin]] to read, 
		- [[Merric]] is convinced this really [[Nand]] and that he is out of his depth. 
		- [[Merric]] gives in as there isn’t an option.
	- [[Nand]] nods goodbye to the group and causes the spell circle to appear at the bow of the boat. Everyone steps into the circle and the runes of the circle settle and dull slightly. 
		- The threads of [[The Weave]] starts to become tangible and feed into the sphere. The threads tighten as if being pulled on and the ball of energy wraps itself around [[Ultrin]] like a shawl. 
		- [[Nand]] lets out a pained exhale and says “that’s it, you have it now”. [[Ultrin]] feels like he is concentrating on something incomprehensible that is far bigger than him, connected directly to[[The Weave]], with infinite power at his fingertips. [[Ultrin]] feels that he can shape [[The Weave]] into a weapon.
		- There is a feeling of building pressure as the space around us is compressed. The star in front of us reacts violently, the worms erupting from the start become huge as the star explodes. 
	- The ship begins to groan as pressure builds from below the deck, causing it to buckle upwards with a sound like bone tearing and wood splintering.
		- A vertical wound appears through the Center of the ship. Worms begin to pour out of it like a reversed waterfall, knitting into a vast gaping maw in the ship. 
		- [[Jorund]]  notices that in the throat of the gaping maw is a knotted mass of writing flesh, subtly glowing with a sickly green light and pulsating like a heart. 
		- From the heart fires forth 2 zombie-worm spawn of [[Kyusse]] that crawl onto the deck. 2 tendrils of worms wrap themselves around the edge of the ship. 
	- Wisps of magical energy, clearing the spores from our lungs and the worm spawn rush towards the prow.
		- One of the creatures hits [[Jorund]], the other misses [[Merric]]
		- Three more worm creatures emerge. 
		- The tentacles moves and attack [[Jorund]] and [[Merric]].
		- [[Aust]] casts spike growth next to the heart. 
		- The heart uses and a wave of necrotic energy passes over us doing much damage
		- A meteor strikes the deck, hitting [[Aust]] and [[Jorund]]. It leaves behind a residue of blue/green flame.
		- Merric gets hit by a worm creature and a worm starts to bury into him
		- Merric summons shadows to the tentacle and hits it with his sword, and a psychic screams lashes out.
		- [[Aust]] casts mass cure wounds, then the heart beats out a necrotic blast.
	- The star screams, [[Ultrin]] struggles to hold onto the spell
	- [[Ultrin]] struggles to aim the spell, whips out the book and starts to chant another spell from that book. The power of the aimed spell seems to redirect and refocus on the star. All of the threads of [[The Weave]] coil in and condense. 
		- [[Ultrin]] feels that he holds a small element of the spell to himself. 
		- A psychic scream of a dying beast emanates from the star. Worms larger than comprehension scatters into deep space. 
		- A wound forms on the surface of the start as it recoils, forced back by a power it doesn’t understand.
		- All the foes ahead of us disintegrate.
		- The remainder of [[the Weave]] flows into the book, which snaps shut. 
		- The ship steadies itself and [[The Weave]] remnants left behind condense into [[Ultrin]] and his connection to it snaps shut, yet he feels some of it is within him. This feels very powerful, but not entirely what he wants.
	- The wound of the surface of the star spreads as it retreats and disappears, vanishing from the far realm. The runes along the perimeter of the ship flicker weakly and continue to dim and then extinguish.
	- Complete darkness surrounds us and the pressure that held us floating suspended vanishes. The ship lurches and is falling. Wind screams past the deck, then light, then clouds. We fall from the sky, back towards the ground. 
		- [[Jorund]] glimpses towers, walls, smoke, it is [[Neverwinter]] but the city is not how we left it. Some portions of the city re in complete darkness. Beyond the walls of the city are incomprehensible shapes moving, where they should not. The barrier around the city looks smaller, moved inwards to protect less of the city.
		- [[Castle Never]] looks scarred, as if attacked by gargantuan creatures. The banners on the castle are flying at half mast. 
		- The ship passes through the barrier towards the river, which is a black green, choked with debris. The ship slams into the river and the river waters explode around us.
