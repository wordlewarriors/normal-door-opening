## In a stained glass memory

- [[Jorund]] is front and centre, [[Merric]] is off to the left, [[Ultrin]] is at the back
	- [[Jorund]] and [[Merric]] realise they are in a church that was converted by the cult of [[Mykrul]] - all of the iconography has been profaned and is clearly very evil
	- [[Mykrul]] was once human and became a lich and then a god. He rose along with Bane and Bhaal. His teachings said that even gods should die, so his followers love that he is now dead. But there is still power here.
- There is a man waiting to begin a sermon. His vestments are frayed, mended with rusty stitching. His eyes are entirely black. The congregation is entirely silent - they aren’t breathing, but seem to be alive.
	- It feels like it takes much effort to move here -like when previously trying to change a vision, there is a force trying to maintain the vision.
- The sermon begins, as the man turns his back to the congregation to face the altar
	- He proclaims: “The first god died smiling, not screaming, not afraid. He did not fall, he dove.” The congregation chants in unison “Down down down down”
	- [[Merric]] can hear under the chanting a low hum, like wings flapping but slowly.
	- “He laid down his crown in the soil, he let the worms take his eyes so that he might see what the other gods cannot“
	- [[Merric]] can hear the hum growing in volume, there is a wetness to the wing sound that is very unsettling and distracting. The speed of wingbeats increases. No one else seems distracted
	- [[Merric]] telepathically alerts [[Ultrin]] & [[Jorund]] - who starts to be able to feel a pulsing ripple that is coming from the altar, like a heart beating in the basement.
	- “The raven watches the passing. [[Kelemvor]] watches the scales, [[Urlogalan]] tends the bonds, but not of them tends what lies beneath the grave. None of them raises the coffin lid, but he does and we do. [[Mykrul]] is not dead, he is death unbound. The gods of death fear death but ours died eons ago. He showed us the rust. He showed us the worm, who coils through tombs, through temples. We found the bones of divinity beneath their cathedrals. We remember what their gods forgot, we were never meant to die, we were meant to return. It is time for us to emerge.“
	- The ritualist is holding a dagger made of the same material as the altar, so tightly that his hand is bleeding. His hands are covered in scars - this isn’t the first time he’s done this.
	- The ritualist raises the dagger and the congregation begins moaning in ecstasy and enjoying it far too much. The air begins to thicken as it seems to grow tainted. The material of the dagger seems to absorb the light around it. 
- [[Jorund]] is holding the hilt of [[Dawnbringer]] which is struggling to manifest against the darkness, but is being suppressed by this place.
- A sound like a shrieking gale echoes through the church as the grand front doors are thrown open. Hulking warriors, swearing armour adorned with scorched black feathers and with tattoos stride in. Their leader launches an orb of divine power at the ritualist and instructs his men to purge this place.
	- The air ruptures with divine force as the inquisitors cut through corpses and move towards the altar. 
	- In desperation the ritualist begins to plunge the dagger towards the alter, the heartbeat grows louder and appears to come from the alter. The heartbeat can be felt throughout your body
- In instant before the dagger strikes the altar the world stops, except for the 3 of us. Looking around, everyone else is frozen in time. There is some from the incense suspended in the air. There is a silence so complete it is physically oppressive. 
	- There is the sound of a rustle, like the quiet turning of the page of a book. From the shadows above the alter a raven descends. It is larger than any you have seen and its black feathers shimmer with a starry night quality. One wing is bound in white cloth and stained with a dark blood like colour. It perches on the alter next to the ritualist.
	- [[Jorund]] hears in his mind a voice neither kind nor cruel: “You would stop the blade, I know. But this soul is already written, it was meant to die here, to stop it is to rupture the grand design, to unmake the thread that tethers fate to silence”
	- The raven approaches and [[Jorund]] feels the hilt of [[Dawnbringer]] tremble in his grasp. “Your queen would abandon you, even for this thought of intervening. What are you [[Jorund]], if not a man of thoughts you never say out loud”
	- The raven hops to one side and on the alter is an obsidian coin with veins of ash running through it.
- [[Jorund]] approaches the alter and looks at the coin. It is larger than regular currency. Probably valuable. The face up side is showing a skull.
	- There are stories told in the [[Raven Queen]] clergy what happens when you die. One such claims that there is no way to prove yourself worthy and she has no interest in merit as faith is pre-ordinated, instead she simply flips a coin.
	- [[Jorund]] suspects this is the coin of the boy, within the box, who probably died. [[Jorund]] could potentially change fate, which the [[Raven Queen]] would hate. 
	- The two giant stained glass windows behind the altar flicker to life, showing a triptych of a young boy’s future 
		- As a cleric of the comman man - ministering to farmers and giving funerals.
		- Using his powers to reanimate a fallen friend. Over time this power corrupts, going from a fallen friend to raising an army of the dead
		- The boy becomes more decayed by worms - his body, his wounds and his eyes - until he sits upon a throne of worms
	- The voice intones “Perhaps he learned to give the dead names. Perhaps it began with love and power corrupted. Or perhaps when you break fate, something takes note. Had the boy lived, his future is unknown. That is what scares us.”
- [[Jorund]] ensures [[Dawnbringer]] is secure in its sheath, switching out its light, and then goes to sit back down in his seat, watching the raven and waiting.
	- [[Jorund]] can feel a closeness from [[Dawnbringer]] and feels a thousand sensations and sees the moment he took her from the pool of sunlight, when he achieved great things and yet went unrewarded, if not punished. 
	- She feels abject terror of the heartbeat from the altar. 
	- She feels a terror of what it would mean for her if [[Jorund]] were to change fate and she were instead a dying torch held by a man who turned his back on the gods.
- The magic holding this space begins to dissipate as time picks up again. The ritualist stabs the altar and the dagger disappears from his hand. There is a feeling of great evil in this place. The weave is strong, but not in a good way
	- The guiding bolt of holy power hits the ritualist who goes flying into the darkness.
	- There is a different wet noise, like some great beast eating.
- The rest of the events play out as [[Jorund]] remembers, with everyone being cut down. [[Jorund]] is arrested and the rest of the party is ignored.
	- [[Dawnbringer]] tries to convince themself and [[Jorund]] that this was the right choice.
- The altar fractures, outside the cathedral there is a pulse of bright blood red as a terrible shriek echoes through the place and shatters the glass.
	- There is a feeling that this entire place groans in pain. The sky shatters like glass, reviewing a world of impossible geometry, a sun that casts shadow and a plain of black stone. Ahead is a tower reaching into the sky, its battlements slick with blood.
	- [[Merric]] thinks that this is the divine domain of the [[Raven Queen]]. Which seems broadly good, but also a place that is very unsafe. The walls of the fortress are trembling under an unseen pressure. Below the platform we stand on in a giant valley is something vast and rotting that is shifting
	- The rotten mass is actually a huge army of the undead, pressing towards the gates of the fortress with some unifying hunger, moving as if a single entity, lead by banner less knights. They appear to be winning.
- Standing at the edge of the platform, watching this horror is a figure. A tall, figure, clad in tattered shrouds of cloth, facing away, giving the impression of a faithful of the [[Raven Queen]] who would be in mourning. It is/was wingéd, the single remaining wing is tattered and broken, with the remaining feathers rotten. The other wing is missing, with a faintly visible shadow of where it used to be. They appear unarmed. Without moving, they speak:
	- ”The queen does not await you in peace.” [[Jorund]] asks if he must cross. “That isn’t something I can choose for you. The Queen’s sanctum is breached, the undead rise, there are too many dead and too few to come. The battle is already lost”
	- “The battle can’t be lost here. It wont be lost here. What would become of the world without the mistress”
	- “That is her fate and we must not intervene in such things”
	- “It doesn’t have to be her fate. Some things aren’t written yet”
	- “Perhaps. We fought, we fell”
	- “But she is still fighting”
	- “She does not fight, she awaits you”
	- “It sounds like my fate is to get there, regardless of what happens afterwards. It looks like you could once fly. That would be really helpful right now. What’s your plan? Stand and wait?”
	- “I am one of the Queen’s watchers. It is my job to watch this unfold. I do not wish to but I must.“
	- “I don’t have that job. I’m not sure what job I do have, but its not that one”
	- “When you are ready, you will be able to meet with her” The figure turns to face [[Jorund]]. “Are you ready?”
	- “I have already died, but meeting the [[Raven Queen]] is the end. She’s unlikely to send you back”
	- “I do not know her plans. I know she awaits you. I know not why. That is for her to know”
- [[Jorund]] asks if the watcher can control the platform, it turns out they can. “If you are now ready, I shall summon the path. Once you step upon it, the queen will know that you have arrived and there will be no turning back”. It presses a clawed hand into the platform, which shudders, then a bridge of onyx and pale marble appears from nowhere, defying gravity and building a path to the fortress.
	- “When you walk this path, you are in her hands.”
	- [[Merric]] and [[Ultrin]] reluctantly follow.
- Walking over the bridge and looking down, there appear to be skeletal dragons in the army and giant worms slithering their way up the fortress. This isn’t an army you could fight and nobody seems to be resisting them - as if the army of the [[Raven Queen]] has already fallen.
	- The bridge ends and we hop onto the battlements. The bridge immediately falls. [[Merric]] is making his way stealthily along.
	- A giant wooden door bars the way to the inside. [[Jorund]] pushes it open and it opens with ease, showing a grand room of solid marble. In the Center is a throne made of intricately carved marble feathers, upon which sits a figure that we assume is the [[Raven Queen]], obscured in shadows. 
	- It is cold in the room as wind whips past, but doesn’t feel bad. Her voice cuts through: “You chose the cycle. You chose fate. This pleases me.” She rises and the veil of shadows lifts enough to show dark endless eyes. “There are too few who obey without embellishment. I see too many blades dulled by guilt or indecision. You are not one of them.”
	- The figure walks towards [[Jorund]], reaching out to touch his forehead as he bows slightly. 
		- As she reaches out to finger him
	- She says “You are mine. You will go where I cannot, you will do what I must not do. When the threads of fate begin to fray, you will cut them without hesitation. ”
	- The fortress feels as if it is beginning to collapse.
	- “Go now [[Jorund]], I name you The Watcher’s Blade” The feeling of reluctance, distance and regret that [[Jorund]] felt now drops and he feels a deep connection to the powers of the [[Raven Queen]].
	- She turns her back and the veil of shadows drops, making her look uncomfortably like a human woman. Her throne is consumed by swirling black rotten feathers tipped with frost. There is the sound of countless wings that grows to the point of being deafening, then silence. 
- After a sudden blink we are back on the boat. Cold mist swirls around us, like the feeling in the throne room of the [[Raven Queen]]
	- The boat has once again grown in all directions and is made of a nicer material. The wood is no longer rotten, instead shiny and intricately carved. [[The Ferryman]] stands at the helm, the oar that became a staff is embellished with gems and a magical shimmer - but is still used as an oar. We are now above the river.
	- There is some form of updraft that is causing the boat to lift.
- [[Ultrin]] is reminded of his days when he was a student at the academy. [[Neverwinter]] has a strong shipwright industry - their certain ships have scholars from the academy ward their ships. To do so requires to painstakingly turn the entire thing into a rune. This ship has the same sort of style - the signature of [[Neverwinter Academy]]. There appears to be a set of glyphs that allows the ship to float; generally over the waves to make travel easier - not to fly.
	- [[The Ferryman]] is staring into the distance. He looks like [[Jorund]], but with nicer and cleaner clothes. When we first met him, he looked a bit of a rough customer, perhaps undead or demonic. Now the Feywild green of one eye is matched with the coalesced shadow of the [[Shadowfell]] in the other. His robes are now middle to upperclass and he has skin.
- [[Jorund]] attempts to use [[Dawnbringer]] but she refuses to manifest. He doesn’t force the issue.
- [[Merric]] talks to [[The Ferryman]] - he seems more with it, but still doesn’t know who he is/was. He sounds different - more educated and less plain spoken
