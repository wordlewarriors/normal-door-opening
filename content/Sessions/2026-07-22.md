## 🎲 Recap
While Ultrin had his halo calibrated, Aust gathered seeds from the academy, planted them in the Castle Never atrium, and cast plant growth on them, feeling his connection to Silvanus strengthen. In the tunnels, Aust and Ultrin ritually detected the magic woven into the inverted barrier, and once Jorund ruled out illusion, the party fought through a wordle-style puzzle under mounting pressure until Ultrin stumbled through into the corrupted section beyond. There they found the tunnel narrowing under thick, veined fungus that pulsed like a heartbeat and smelled sweet and warm, and pressed on stealthily with Aust and Neverember leading. They reached an impossible chamber, absent from the blueprints, holding four small webbed mushroom captives guarded by four larger violet-capped mushrooms around a pulsing fungal mass — and a face woke in the wall's fungus, spoke into their minds, and the party beat a hasty retreat.
