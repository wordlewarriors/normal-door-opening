## Awakening afterwards on the [[River Styx]]

- The sound of ominous whistling grows closer, there is the feeling of a cold circle on our chests
- [[Merric]] feels something grabbing him, like a force enveloping and moving him in a different direction, then a feeling of a rough surface on his back, and feeling like he is physical again and can move by himself. 
	- He begins to hear and see normally and sees he is on a small boat (a skiff). Movement still feels awkward. There is a hooded figure standing, with a pair of red glowing eyes from beneath their hood. The left hand is held outwards, the other holds a rotten wooden ore.
	- [[Merric]]’s memories feel unordered, but he feels like he died as his soul crossed over. He is reminded of the tale of the river Styx and [[The Ferryman]].
- [[The Ferryman]]  waves his hand towards the “water” and it feels like something else appears - [[Ultrin]] is suddenly onboard the boat.
- [[The Ferryman]] waves his hand again and [[Jorund]] appears. He does it once more and [[Aust]] appears.
- [[Merric]] very stiffly stands up, finding the boat is far more stable than he expects. Looking around, there is no horizon. Looking down below the boat, there are more than just twinkles of light - there are faces and bodies, some distorted, some posed at rest. [[The Ferryman]] is staring at him.
	- [[Merric]] steps towards [[The Ferryman]] - he seems scared of the group. The red eyes and skeletal gaunt face of [[The Ferryman]] has an expression of concern. 
	- [[Merric]] picks up a gold coin that feel to his feet when he stood up. His backpack is missing, the only thing in his pockets is a crystal. It appears to be some kind of spell casting conduit/focus. 
- [[Aust]] sits up and says hello to [[The Ferryman]], who seems confused by his presence. It responds “greetings traveller, this skiff  is not built for more than 1, it usually only carries 1, what binds you to these souls?”
	- [[Aust]] explains that the group was in a fight and chose to sacrifice something. [[The Ferryman]] is surprised, this is new to him. 
	- [[Aust]] asks where we are, is told the [[River Styx]]. [[The Ferryman]] is unsure where we are being taken, says that he is only here to ward off the anomalies of the river. 
	- [[The Ferryman]] asked what our destination is and what we seek to achieve. [[Aust]] describes the fighting between the gods, says he seeks to restore the balance. 
	- [[The Ferryman]] describes the souls of the [[Merric]] and [[Ultrin]] as tainted - not entirely their own.
- [[Jorund]] tries asking where this journey began - [[The Ferryman]] tells him that he found us in the river. He says that the gods have no power upon the river. 
- [[The Ferryman]] asks where we want to go, we all agree that we are here to continue the fight, but don’t know where.
- [[Aust]] is curious about the source of the taint on the souls. We rule out spell plague as [[Jorund]] has that too. Suggests that it is due to [[Ivog]]. [[The Ferryman]] looks concerned at the mention of the [[Chosen of Null]] - a death god.
- [[Jorund]] asks whether he could get a separate boat with [[Aust]], but is told they are all linked together.
- [[The Ferryman]] asks whether [[Merric]] is prepared for forces to try to claim the remainder of his soul. [[Merric]] complains he doesn’t have his weapons, to which [[The Ferryman]] states that the river provides what is needed.
- [[Aust]] turns to [[Ultrin]] and asks what could be his problem. [[Ultrin]] turns into a shadow - [[Merric]] notices that this is the second spell [[Ultrin]] has been able to cast, when he wasn’t able to summon a pact weapon.
	- [[Aust]] asks [[Ultrin]] why he cast the spell. [[Ultrin]] claims that he is stuck with the book. [[Aust]] convinces him to show the book to [[The Ferryman]].
- [[The Ferryman]] says it’s clear why [[Merric]]’s soul is displaced, but he asks [[Ultrin]] if he seeks godhood. [[The Ferryman]] claims the journey will progress through all the planes, down to the astral sea, where pollution is ejected and may be where the river ends up throwing [[Ultrin]].
	- [[Aust]] speculates that the longer [[Ultrin]] has the book, the more of his soul will be lost to it. 
	- [[Ultrin]] thinks that reading the book will explain the answers. [[Merric]] has heard tales of wizards becoming a lich by severing their soul from their body - moving their soul piece by piece into a phylactery.
	- [[Ultrin]] reads the book some more, he says it doesn’t contain anything relevant about relationship. [[Merric]] looks closely and other than it looks like this is the first time [[Ultrin]] has read it, nothing else happens. 
- [[Merric]] looks at the gold coin he woke up with. It appears to be created by magic, not like any currency he’s ever seen, it has no writing or emblems on it and is perfectly plain. 
- [[Merric]] tries to grab the spell book from [[Ultrin]], but he turns into a shadow. [[Ultrin]] claims that he’s going to prove that he doesn’t need it, by throwing it away, except he finds himself unable to. [[Merric]] suggests he can just put the book down, but even that seems beyond [[Ultrin]].
- [[Ultrin]] casts remove curse to try and remove attunement to the book - which succeeds. He then drops the book over the side and asks [[The Ferryman]] if this has affected his soul - is told no.
- [[The Ferryman]] says not all make it to the end of their journey - not all souls are worthy, not all want to make it, or some issues are unresolvable. 
- [[Ultrin]] seems to pull his book out of thin air as shadow comes in from all directions around the boat and coagulates into the book. 
- [[Ultrin]] has a has a symbol of Ohgma, [[Aust]] has a spring of mistletoe, [[Jorund]] has a symbol of the [[Raven Queen]].
- [[Merric]] tries to cast a spell and fails. He then tries to cast a spell that [[Ivog]] gave him, recalling how it used to work for [[Ivog]]. The spell doesn’t work but there is a feeling in the back of [[Merric]]’s mind that it may somewhere, somehow be possible. 
	- Looking at the crystal sphere, it‘s light blue, opaque-ish but peering into it, in the centre of it is blackened, as if charred on the inside.
	- [[Merric]] asks [[The Ferryman]] about the sphere, he replies that there are some things a soul cannot part with. [[The Ferryman]] suggests that the journey might explain it. 
- [[Merric]] asks [[The Ferryman]] about payment and why he has gained a coin. [[The Ferryman]] explains that the river sometimes directs him to pickup things lost in the river. 

## Later on the [[River Styx]]
- As [[The Ferryman]] drops his hand occasionally, there is a strong sense of a loud wind being kept at bay. 
- Eventually the river is not just a void on the horizon, whirlpools emitting white light appear. Some the river drains into, others is spills out of - [[Ultrin]] identifies this as Pandamonium, the plane of a thousand portals (most of them just link to other areas of the plane).
	- The river turns a slight tint of green, deepening to a deep jade and then a deep blue - looking like water.
	- The skiff seems to hit something under the water and scrapes against wood as the boat crashes into the shore of a forest. There is a wall of trees, vines, thorns head.
	- [[The Ferryman]] drops his hand and there is no wind, “Ah, your journey begins at the roots, as all things do”. He waves his hand at the roots and they part.
- [[Aust]] leads the group in heading into the forest. Getting off the boat, [[Merric]] notices that [[Aust]] appears to shrink, becoming shorter and less muscular before beckoning to the group to follow him. [[Aust]] seems to look very young (around 14? 15?). 
	- Everyone starts to feel/seem younger as they travel down the forest path and everyone seems Elven.
