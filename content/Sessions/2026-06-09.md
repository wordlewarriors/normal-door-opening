## 🎲 Recap
At [[Neverwinter Academy]], [[Professor History]] — a war-forged scholar flanked by two siege golems — dispersed the crowd and ushered [[Ultrin]] and [[Aust]] inside. [[Magister Eloin Vale]] warned against public debate and escorted the party to the laboratory housing the portable ward. The device, which had never behaved consistently for anyone, immediately reacted to [[Ultrin]]: its runes rewrote themselves, the barrier activated around him, and the crystal followed him as if bonded — though the link severed at distance. [[Dean Maverin Soul]] declared that magic had somehow chosen [[Ultrin]] and requested 45 minutes to calibrate the device before it is carried to [[Neverdeath Graveyard]]. Meanwhile at the [[House of Knowledge]], [[Jorund]] and [[Merric]] learned that corrupting funerary rites requires witnesses and a genuine believer's prayer — with disturbing parallels to becoming a lich — received blueprints and tunnel maps for the elven and halfling mausoleums, and were then escorted to meet the [[Grand Scribe]].

## [[Neverwinter Academy]]
- [[Ultrin]] is approached by some academics who ask a variety of questions about the debate that [[Ultrin]] promised, where he's been, what the star is, who's in charge. One gets ready to note down his answers.
	- [[Ultrin]] begins to feel a rumble in his feet and sees the doors of [[Neverwinter Academy]] are open. There is a 7/8ft tall war forged walking towards the crowd. His body is fashioned from steel and stone, with brass joints holding it together. He is designed to look human yet clearly isnt. He wears a scholar's mantle on his shoulders. He has no mouth and 2 points of light for eyes. This is [[Professor History]] - the head of necromancy. 
	- [[Professor History]] is flanked by 2 war golems - constructs somewhat like him, but 10ft tall. They have large hammers on each arm. They have channels built into each arm for holding spells, but there appears to be nothing in them. The crowd makes space before their advance.
	- [[Ultrin]] remembers these golems are generally kept inactive, within [[Neverwinter Academy]], against any time that the academy ever goes to war. These golems are enchanted to never run out of power and are built for sieges. They have not had to be deployed in many lifetimes. They aren't meant for crowd control, they are for fighting other cities.
- [[Professor History]] proclaims that the proposed discourse has not been scheduled. He gestures for [[Ultrin]] and [[Aust]] to go inside. [[Ultrin]] decides to go inside, of his own free will.
	- The grand entry hallway has statues of each of the previous grandmasters, made in different styles over time. 
	- [[Aust]] takes in the sights.
- Standing in the entrance hall is [[Magister Eloin Vale]]. She complains to [[Ultrin]] that he's only been here a minute and has promised the city a public reckoning, they should discuss this. [[Ultrin]] contends that he only promised this to the wizards!
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] tries to explain to [[Ultrin]] that he doesn't have to give everyone the answers that they seek. She tells him that answers should be agreed behind closed doors and then conveyed, without exposing the fracturing of [[Neverwinter Academy]] in public
	- [[Ultrin]] wants to make visible the views of the crowd so their views can be addressed.
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] ushers [[Ultrin]] towards the laboratory to discuss [[Neverdeath Graveyard]]. [[Ultrin]] requests information about what has been happening here.
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] explains that the protective ward is in the laboratory and that the entire council is wanting to see how the portable barrier will react to [[Ultrin]]
- There are faculty arguing over things as [[Ultrin]] and [[Aust]] walk through [[Neverwinter Academy]]. They seem to be actively prepairing defences for all of the windows. There is the sound of metal hitting metal, which [[Ultrin]] believes to be the sound of the war golems being repositioned on reinforced walkways. 
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] explains that [[Neverwinter Academy]] is putting itself on a war footing and reinforcing its defences. 
	- [[Aust]] points out that there are hairline cracks in the walls of [[Neverwinter Academy]]. [[Ultrin]] knows this should be **very** hard to do as the walls are magically reinforced. [[Aust]] also spots rooms with crops grown in them. The crops aren't just dead or withered, they have grown wrong - they appear to have grown away from the water. This upsets [[Aust]] and he interrogates [[Ultrin]] on the forced growth of plants. [[Ultrin]] deflects the question to [[Magister Eloin Vale]] who explains that the city needs to be sustained somehow and natural cycles are taking too long. She is unsure why the plants are dying. There is fungal rot in the soil outside the city, but the soil in [[Neverwinter Academy]] should be seperate and no rot has been found here. It appears that the magic being used is causing them to grow wrongly.  There are fewer crops every day. [[Ultrin]] reminds her to try growing crops at the castle - she is reticent to overcommit to growing too much there. [[Ultrin]] asks for this to be expedited. 
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] mentions that some of the fungal rot has attached itself to the outside of [[Neverwinter Academy]]. [[Ultrin]] speculates that this could be part of the cause of the cracks in the walls. [[Magister Eloin Vale]] says that some speculate that this is because the magic used has altered and become less effective. 
- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] explains that [[Lord Neverember]] tasked [[Neverwinter Academy]] to develop a portable ward that could be used to leave [[Neverwinter]] and take part of the barrier with them. It doesn't work as magic didn't used to work this way. There has been much effort applied to getting the magic to work, rather than simply being a fixed centre. Current efforts are on enchanting a portable item. Some believe that [[Ultrin]] will be able to help as he has seen [[The Weave]]
	- [[Ultrin]] thinks this isnt the best use of his time.
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] explains that the barrier around [[Neverwinter Academy]] seems to be moving by itself at times and seems to be almost expressing desires. This shouldn't be possible. The barrier appears to react differently based on who is working with it. It seems to "trust" some more than others, but this seems to change over time. There was a pattern until a day ago, but now it's inconsistent.
	- [[Ultrin]] feels a wave of pity for [[Neverwinter Academy]] flow through him. The academy has learned that magic is malleable and they use it to create a small shield, when they could be doing *so much more…* It should be possible to expand on things like delayed blast fireball and make a bomb? Why bother with a shield when you could make a weapon? [[Ultrin]] feels something at the back of his mind pushing him in this direction.
- [[Ultrin]] asks [[Magister Eloin Vale]] what research has been done into [[Nihal]] and mentions the sight of a beating heart in time with the funeral bells. 
	- She explain some believe the bells are holding the star here but either the focus being generated is either holding the star in place and empowering it, or is keeping the star at bay. She also explains that the star is slowly drawing the planet towards it. The planet is already orbiting 2 stars and now there's 3! Which makes everything very complex. It appears that approaching the star is causing the motion to accelerate, but its unclear how much this is happening. 
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] notes that staring at the star for too long seems to ingender negative emotions over time - fear, anger. 
- [[Ultrin]] asks if there has been research in to blowing up the star. One of the members of staff is very in favour of finishing [[Nand]]'s work and destroying the star. 
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] points out that there's a risk that the ruins of the star would then crash down on the city,
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] explains that this course of action is unpopular in the academy, but that taking any action is likely preferable to none.
- [[Ultrin]], [[Aust]] and [[Magister Eloin Vale]] arrive at the laboratory. [[Ultrin]] looks in through a reinforced window while [[Magister Eloin Vale]] unlocks the 70 different locks on the door.
	- [[Ultrin]] can see a suspended framework around a fist sized blue crystal that is hovering, surrounded by 6 silver ribs, with blue-white light flickering between them. There are 3 warded concentric circles positioned to keep the barrier in place (one should be sufficient). The structure seems to contract and there are crystal lenses that focusing light onto it. Several of the lenses seem to turn towards [[Ultrin]], seeming to express an interest that magic should not be able to do.
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] gasps as she sees this, exclaiming that it's never done that before! She explains that the outer ring is to prevent the barrier expanding too far. The middle ring is to measure the barrier. The inner ring is part of the device and is the intended size of the barrier. The barrier light stretches between the ribs, with the crystal being the anchor. The ribs can be expanded to control the size of the barrier (but if expanded too far the barrier will collapse).
- At the far side of the lab is [[Dean Maverin Soul]] (a member of the temporary council - believes [[Neverwinter Academy]] should be transported elsewhere). [[Professor History]] is also present, observing, as are various research students. They are all hyper focused on the apparatus, for the arrival of [[Ultrin]]. No-one is near the prototype. 
	- [[Magister Eloin Vale]]  begins addressing the room, explaining the barrier is to go outside the barrier proper and can pass through it without joining the greater whole. It can be carried by a mounted unit and will repel the [[Starspawn of Nihal]]. If compromised, the barrier should collapse safely (without crushing anyone within it).
	- Experiments to date have failed by anchoring the barrier to the floor and being immobile; by moving of its own accord as if it wants to join the larger barrier; it once expanded to larger than expected; it sometimes forms an uneven shape in an echo of the greater barrier; it has tried to merge itself with [[Neverwinter Academy]]. Since magic doesn't have will, none of this should have happened. The portable barrier has not so far been consistently both. 
	- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] complains at the characterisation of magic as having desires. Despite this, [[Ultrin]] gets a sense of emotion from the barrier - it reminds him of a caged animal that does not want to be. It seems to have emotions.
	- [[Ultrin]] sees that the runes used to create the barrier are generally conventional but are incomplete, as those used to anchor to a point are missing - this is an attempt to create something new.
	- [[Aust]] is begins to cast detect magic, which is gathering attention from the crowd. 
- [[Magister Eloin Vale]] asks [[Ultrin]] to see if he can control the barrier, since it seems to be reacting to him. She and [[Aust]] back away.
	- [[Ultrin]] doesn't feel in danger but isnt sure what the barrier will do. He approaches the outer circle. The crystal within the device rotates in place and it feels as if something is turning to look at him. There is an erratic hum from the ribs which stabilises into a somewhat pleasant note as they harmonise with each other. All the researchers are frantically scribbling notes.
	- [[Ultrin]] approaches the middle barrier. The ribs rotate from vertical to a perfect circle around the crystal and start to glow with the same light as the great barrier. A faint outline of a barrier appears around the innermost ring - it looks like the device has started working.
	- [[Ultrin]] looks at the runes of the device and sees that the runes appear to be at breaking point as if overpowered and the runes on the ribs appear to be changing, as if the spell is re-writing itself as it forms into place - which should be impossible.
	- [[Ultrin]] approaches edge of the barrier, which appears to strengthen in power, but appears to still be incomplete (and would not repel him). The ribs are approaching perfect alignment.
	- [[Ultrin]] walks through the barrier and approaches the crystal. The second he does so, the metal ribs snap into perfect alignment and the barrier becomes a real, functional barrier. All the runes on the circles in the room go dark and the ward projected from the crystal seems to move. 
	- [[Ultrin]] senses that the barrier has shifted, so its focus is now being projected around him, as the crystal hovers over his head. [[Ultrin]] reaches up to grab the crystal but is unable to move it. He attempts to mentally command the crystal to diminish the barrier - which succeeds. The sphere collapses and the ribs realign and fold around the crystal, which hovers behind [[Ultrin]], resting just behind his neck and following him around.
	- [[Ultrin]] commands the device to stay - at this the device reforms itself and returns to the centre of the room. The 3 circles in the floor remain dark. 
	- [[Ultrin]] moves away from the device and then calls it towards him - nothing happens. [[Ultrin]] feels very embarrassed by the knowledge that multiple researchers have recorded this failure. He feels like his connection to the device has been severed by being so far away.
- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] walks into the room: "Very curious, you can command it when nobody else can. I said that magic has no will but I feel I was wrong. It seems the barrier has chosen you. Magic should not have such choice and yet it has decided."
	- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] asks for time to do final calibrations for 45min before [[Ultrin]] takes it with him to [[Neverdeath Graveyard]]. He suggests that [[Ultrin]] speak with the council while this happens.
	- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] hopes to make the device more predictable and to be able to form a remote link to be able to repair the device in the field and ensure that if the barrier collapses it does so safely.

	- Turning to the next mausoleum, it's for halflings. It is otherwise similar to the first mausoleum. There is a chamber with a central stone slab, and a lower crypt exposed to the earth.

This wouldn't go all the way, but would get most of the way into the graveyard and would be possible to dash to our destination. One exit would be that the tunnels connect directly to the elven mausoleum. It could be also possible to exit pretty close to the [[Raven Queen]] fortified position.
