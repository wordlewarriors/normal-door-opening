## 🎲 Recap
[[Aust]] cured the corrupted Myconids, removing the violet rot, and [[Ashwake]] shared memories with him through her spores, including how the violet fungus forced its way into the greater Myconid consciousness. [[Ashwake]] agreed to travel with the party and explained that the face in the tunnel was a Myconid sovereign whose spores are under attack. After resting in [[Ultrin]]'s magical hut, the party followed the tunnel to the eastern edge of [[Neverdeath Graveyard]], where the ground heaved with fungus, sarcophagi stood broken and the [[Starspawn of Nihal]] wheeled overhead. [[Ultrin]] and [[Merric]] crossed to an iron access hatch, opened it with the bell sequence and found a tunnel of bones and torn fungus. [[Lord Neverember]] and the rest followed hand in hand, and the session ended with a face forming in the fungus ahead.
