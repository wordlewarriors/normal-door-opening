## In a cave in a vision

- Nearby there are numerous rocks with runes carved into them. There is also a piece of silken shroud. There is a dried crimson herb in a bowl. There is also a disk of black wax incense, a bone dagger and a jar with translucent yellow-green fluid (with a worm). 
- To [[Merric]] the ritual seems almost real, but not quite. The implication to [[Ultrin]] is that a ritual that is close enough but is used to place a worm as the seed of the ritual will then allow the person to cross into SoulEarth but will be tainted.
- [[Merric]]’s strange old form ([[Otho]]) feels tired and aching. He fails to escape from his bonds. 
- [[Jorund]] looks at the runes, they appear to be magical symbols related to souls. He knows the rules should be placed facing the centre. 
- As [[Jorund]] chants the ritual and the runes light up
- [[Merric]] can tell which of his companions are here in the vision. They each have their correct race and are all wearing hooded cultist robe. [[Jorund]]’s has a gold trim and his collar bone shows evidence of tattoos. 
- As [[Jorund]] wraps the cloth around [[Merric]]’s head, [[Merric]] begins to feel a heavy sleep. He tries to give in to the sleep but even that is beyond him.
- [[Jorund]] lights the herb next to [[Merric]] and leaves behind a dense cloud of smoke that seems to contain images of children playing and other joyful memories, as well as some halfling figure walking away from a tree, which causes the smell to move from sweet to foul.
- The worm in the jar begins to pulse and squirm. It becomes more agitated when near [[Otho]]’s face. [[Jorund]] angles the jar so that the worm swims out of the jar and lands on [[Otho]]’s face. It squirms towards the mouth. [[Jorund]] makes a hole in the cloth with his ceremonial dagger. [[Merric]] feels the worm land in his mouth and move under his tongue. 
- The other 3 chant aloud in unison. [[Otho]] sighs and the world around them all groans in pain. The stone slab beneith him cracks and the crack spreads across the floor, walls and ceiling. Everyone begins to fall.
- [[Merric]] feels as if he were entering [[SoulEarth]] and suddenly feels closer to [[Urogalan]], except this feels all wrong, as only the dead should feel this. Suddenly, sleep overcomes everyone as they feel weak and fade - ==except [[Aust]], who’s immune to sleep and sees a group of gnomes moving around the stage set.== 
- Everyone finds themself in a chamber so large it feels like it can’t be real, the edges vanishing into a haze and the roof lost to shadows. In front of the group are 2 towering obelisks, separated by a gulf of cracked bedrock. Suspended between them is a massive scale made of bone, with only a curved spine holding the 2 plates together. The scale keeps tilting first one way then the other and with every movement the ground trembles
	- The Obelisk on the left is jet black, marred by claw marks and an obsidian greatsword almost as great as it is changed to it, floating in front of it, restrained by the chains and suspended by some unknown energy. The surface of the Obelisk appear to depict Merric in various acts of vengeance and generally overcome with rage - burning villages and taking down gods in a bid to take his vengeance.
	- The Obelisk to the right is perfectly smooth and grey. There is a massive black hound curled up at its base. The Obelisk is cared with a peaceful [[Merric]] guiding spirits, restoring names to the dead who suffered undeath and bringing peace to others. 
	- [[Merric]] senses an oppressive aura coming from the huge sword that reminds him of [[Ivog]]. The “dog” seems awake, but peaceful. 
- [[Ultrin]]’s knowledge of gods, death and souls, feels like a soul facing a trial. [[Merric]] seems to be the soul being judged, but it’s unclear how this happens. There is a power here that feels like the power of gods - stronger than [[Ultrin]] will ever be. Except no one is here to judge [[Merric]]
- [[Merric]] sees a raised Dias ahead of him, of unclear purpose, likely a place of judgement. The Dias feels familiar to him and reminds him of home and memories of halfling funerals. There seems to be some significance to it but it was never his role before - this fell within [[Jungo]]’s responsibility. 
- [[Merric]] begins to try and carve his name onto the stone, but he is unable to even scratch it. 
- [[Jorund]] starts to feel a compulsion to judge [[Merric]] and to speak. Memories of [[Merric]] start to run through his mind - times that [[Merric]] tried to save him, the time [[Merric]] stabbed him in the back (literally).
	- [[Jorund]] takes a moment to wonder why he’s still going around with [[Merric]] and ultimately decides that [[Merric]] means well and is ultimately trying to save his family, so he’s probably good at his core. At this pronouncement the rumbling resumes and the scales start to tip towards the right hand grey Obelisk.
- [[Merric]] has a fuzzy memory of a time he needed to speak with [[Jungo]], who had been tasked with preparing burial stones for the clan. He had been chiselling stories into the stone and looking sad but he can’t remember why. 
- The Obelisks are unchanged, their auras seem to be clashing and the peaceful aura seems to be taking control. 
- [[Ultrin]] beings up the small amount of soul harvesting and [[Jorund]] defends [[Merric]] for just getting a little off path. 
	- As they talk about this the scales violently swing back to balance and then tip once again towards peace at [[Jorund]]’s words.
- [[Aust]] begins to feel a compulsion to pass judgement on [[Merric]]. He feels there was a time when [[Merric]] was in a dark place but there was a rescue of his clan and defeat of a dragon. It feels like under his distrustful shell there is a core that cares about family. Many times he could have run, when others were dying, but he chose not to. Plus [[Merric]] did help put [[Aust]]’s dead mother to rest. 
	- The scales try to tip slightly towards the black pillar, but most of the time tip towards the grey pillar.
- The continued judgement helps [[Merric]] remember the funerals over the years and remember why [[Jungo]] was said - that the body is placed into a box and lowered into the earth and become a soul at some time, but in a box! This oppressed his sense of being free to wander and not be confined in the afterlife. [[Merric]] didn’t understand that at the time, but he does now.
- [[Ultrin]] feels the compulsion to pass judgment. [[Ultrin]] feels like [[Merric]] is constantly seeking to cause harm and ways to take his book - which is clearly a bad thing! 
	- The scales move slightly back towards balance, but are still balanced towards the grey pillar. 
	- [[Aust]] chips in that [[Merric]] has had many opportunities to cause [[Ultrin]] harm, including many moments in combat, and yet he didn’t take those. The scales tip slightly towards the grey pillar, yet remains close to balance. 
- [[Merric]] remembers that [[Jungo]] made him promise that his soul wouldn’t be put in a box and that the would be allowed to remain free. [[Jungo]] taught [[Merric]] something about how to carve the burial memories. Suddenly, there is a feeling of being able to reach into memory and grab the chisel. 
	- [[Merric]] remembers how inscribing the memories into the stone sets the soul on that path and commits the soul to those stories, living them out forever. Their soul will empower those who come after and follow their actions.
