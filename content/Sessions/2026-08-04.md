## 🎲 Recap
The party pushed into fungal tunnels warped by [[Araumycos]], finding four captives fused to a wall by violet-veined fungus, watched over by hulking Myconids and a speaking face embedded in the wall. The face spoke of a coming "consummation" and claimed the captives as its children, while the party fought off the guarding Myconids and the pulsing fungal mass at the chamber's centre. [[Aust]] freed one captive, who introduced himself as [[Ashwake]] and began chanting his own name with growing confidence. After the fungal mass and Myconids were destroyed, the wall-face acknowledged defeat, calling the party "curious creatures" who had freed its family from a corrupting demon, and promised they would meet again at the consummation. [[Ashwake]] confirmed he can understand and speak to the party through spores, but says the violet infection in the other captives likely can't be removed.

### ❓ Open Threads
- [[Ashwake]]: freed captive, no longer has a "circle" (family) since it was corrupted; unclear if he can be fully trusted or reintegrated with the party.
- Remaining captives: still bear the violet corruption; party must decide whether to attempt lay on hands or kill them outright.
- The face on the wall: called itself the "blessed brood father" and the captives' "blessed sovereign," vowed to meet the party again "at the consummation" — implies a larger, unresolved event tied to [[Araumycos]].
- "Our lady was wed": the face referenced a wedding and consummation involving an unnamed "lady" — meaning not yet established.
- [[Jorund]]: recalled a "mad cap" legend, a Myconid that dips its head in demon blood for power — possibly relevant to the corrupted Myconids encountered.

### 👥 NPCs This Session

- [[Lord Neverember]] — ally accompanying the party — fought alongside them, invoked [[Nand|Archmage Nandntiersanon]]'s name to rally the killing blow on a Myconid.
