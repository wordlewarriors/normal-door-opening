## 🎲 Recap
Ultrin caught up with Aust, who had gathered numerous seeds and was pushing Neverwinter Academy for full access to study them. Back at the House of Knowledge, Jorund followed Sister Maelin Thorn to Castle Never, handed her the key to the forbidden vault, and set her to work on how to safely dedicate Nand's funeral to Mistra without a body to bury. At Castle Never, Lord Neverember insisted on joining the stealth mission to breach the Starspawn of Nihal despite Aarlon's concerns, and the party learned of a burned-out shop that still somehow trades in rare reagents. A city-wide procession was staged as cover while Merric, Lord Neverember, Aust, Ultrin and Jorund slipped into the House of Knowledge's disused maintenance tunnels. Deep in the moss-choked tunnel they found a whispering, breathing growth, dodged a Myconid, and discovered the barrier itself had already been inverted from within, repelling Ultrin's portable shield.
