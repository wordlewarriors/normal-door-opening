#Swordcoast

- [[Gudrun Rockseeker]] wants a wagon of provisions taken to [[Phandalin]], a few days to the SE, located and recovered - promising everyone 10GP each to take the wagon to the provision shop.
	- On a trail headed east, the wagon is pulled with 2 oxen and is loaded with mining supplies, food, ale, provisions  - overall worth 100GP
- Found the horses head and ambushed by a group of golbins.
- Followed a path to a cave about 5 miles away
	- Started clearing out the cave. Took clothes from a goblin and used it to create a disguise.
	- The Druid and Cleric were both caught in a flash flood of the river through the cave
- The Goblins had a human captive that we eventually saved. This is [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. He is an important looking human from some important order - the [[Griffon Calverly]] of the [[Lords' Alliance]].
	- [[Sildar Hallwinter]] was a companion of [[Gudrun Rockseeker]], who met him in [[Neverwinter]], seeking another [[Lords' Alliance]] member. He wants to restore [[Phandalin]]. He is one of 3 brothers who found the [[Wave Echo Cave]], and formed the [[Phandever's Pact]]. He is a wizard.
	- The [[Wave Echo Cave]] has magical power and can be used to create a forge of spells to create magical items.
	- In the goblin cave a bugbear named [[Clarg]] caught [[Gudrun Rockseeker]]. It was overheard that the [[Black Spider]] is seeking the Ore and has a map to [[Wave Echo Cave]]. [[Gudrun Rockseeker]] was sent to the chief of [[Cragmore]] at [[Cragmore Castle]].
	- [[Sildar Hallwinter]] promises the party 50GP to clear the care and escort him to [[Phandalin]].
- After clearing the cave, the supplies from the wagon are located. The belong to the [[Lion Shield Costa]] out of [[Phandalin]]. Also find a chest with some coin, 2 potions of healing and a jade statue of a frog.
	- [[Wave Echo Cave]] was attacked by Orcs and mercenary wizards. In the process much map of the cavern was damaged and its location was lost.
	- [[Clarg]] answered to [[King Grull]] - the cheild of the [[Cragmore]] tribe. He had a messenger from [[King Grull]] That the [[Black Spider]] wanted [[Gudrun Rockseeker]].
- The supplies were moved back onto the wagon and then taken to [[Phandalin]]

## In [[Phandalin]]
- Found a sign of the [[Red Brands]] on the side of the town hall. They are known to [[Aust]] for chasing people on the road. [[Darren Edomoth]] wanted to fix this
	- We are thanked by the [[Lion Shield Costa]] for delivering the supplies, they were starting to run low. They dont like trading with outsiders. [[Darren Edomoth]] wants to help, he as a cottage out by the orchard.
- Visited [[Barthon's Provisions]] - a much bigger shop than the [[Lion Shield Costa]]
	- We are told that [[Nandrul Rockseeker]] and [[Thordin Rockseeker]] (brothers of [[Gudrun Rockseeker]]) have not been seen in 1-2 weeks. [[Barthon]] is concerned at the organisation of the [[Cragmore]] clan
	- We are told that a Thieves Guild recently established themselves in the town. The local businesses are terrified and the town master has been scared into complicity. The thieves frequent the [[Giant Taphouse]] in town - there's at least 6 of them.
	- [[Darren Edomoth]] is a part of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]] - they protect good people.
	- We are told that the hideout of the [[Red Brands]] must be under the [[Tresendar Manor]] on the hill. There must be 10-15 of them. They sometimes wear crimson capes and tend to stand out.
	- There's a place to the NE called [[Old Owl Well]] - some prospectors from the [[Miners Exchange]] were all chased off by "magic" - they've been silent since and have now left the area.
- There is a valuable heirloom in [[Thundertree]]. The area was threatened by undead years ago and it was left in the family trinket shop - it's now wanted back.
- The [[Red Brands]] are lead by the [[Glasstaff]], who hires all the thugs in town. He lives in barracks hidden behind a hidden door guarded by some sort of a one-eyed monster
	- The monster is a [[Nothic]] and said "one of the party is a demon child"
- The [[Black Spider]] wants to bring back the [[Spellplague]]. They are a worshipper of [[Lolth]].
- The [[Glasstaff]] escaped our raid on [[Tresendar Manor]].
	- [[Lord Albrec]] had a message from the [[Black Spider]] trying to stop us and take any maps that we have.
	- Theory that the [[Glasstaff]] and the [[Black Spider]] are the same person.
	- Over 500 years ago dwarves and gnomes shared [[Wave Echo Cave]]. There are records that a mace called [[Lightbringer]], used by priests of [[Lathander]] was possibly created there, but is now lost within the [[Mines of Phandelver]].
	- Goblins patrol the town and may know of how to reach [[Cragmore Castle]]
- [[Sildar Hallwinter]] promises to find who in the [[Lords' Alliance]] has been working with the [[Black Spider]]. Warns of possible threat of revenge from them.
