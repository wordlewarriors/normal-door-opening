## 🎲 Recap
With [[Nihal]]'s pull dragging the planet starward and every attempt to reach wizards beyond the barrier ending in death, the temporary council — [[Professor History]], [[Thameor Vex]] and [[Dean Maverin Soul]] — laid out their competing plans to [[Ultrin]]: gather more data, finish [[Nand]]'s forbidden work, or transport [[Neverwinter Academy]] itself to restore predictable magic. [[Thameor Vex]] privately urged [[Ultrin]] to follow [[Nand]]'s example and not fear the forbidden, hinting that [[The Party]] are all unfinished pieces of a single spell. [[Ultrin]] reclaimed the bonded [[Portable ward device]], requested a crystal ball, and raided the near-empty academy armoury before setting off for the [[House of Knowledge]]. There, [[Merric]] and [[Jorund]] descended to the [[Great Vault]] and bargained with the [[Grand Scribe]], who halted the destructive redaction, tied the corrupted funerary rites to apotheosis, and handed [[Jorund]] a key naming him custodian of the vault's most dangerous knowledge.

## [[Neverwinter Academy]]
- [[Ultrin]] is lead into an adjacent room by [[Dean Maverin Soul]] ([[Aust]] has wondered off). The 3 members of the temporary council have gathered here - [[Professor History]], [[Thameor Vex]]
	- [[Ultrin]] notices that [[Dean Maverin Soul]] is limping
- [[Ultrin]] summarises that there’s a big star in the sky that’s going to “gobble us all up soon” and summarises the position of each of those present. He believes only one of these addresses the real problem and asks what has been done to contact other wizard organisations elsewhere and if any communications channels are open.
	- [[Professor History]] replies that daily attempts to contact the outside world have failed and the barrier prevents scrying (which causes death by [[Starspawn of Nihal]], within seconds).
	- All have agreed that a portable barrier is needed for research beyond the barrier. The plan was to use a mounted unit for this, but now that [[Ultrin]] has returned and has plans to step outside the barrier anyway… 
- [[Thameor Vex]] is unclear on what [[Nand]] was trying to achieve, but wanted to expose [[Nihal]], not destroy it completely. He believes that [[Ultrin]] and the members of [[The Party]] are all parts of the spell and still have their part to play.
	- [[Ultrin]] asks that he would have to do. [[Thameor Vex]] thinks that [[Nand]] simply passed the plan to [[Ultrin]] thinking that he would know what to do and that by moving the elder evil to the prime material plane it may now be possible to kill it. 
	- [[Thameor Vex]] states that [[Neverwinter Academy]] is no longer the important thing - [[Ultrin]] is! [[Ultrin]] says he wants to solve the “real problem” before they all get “munched”
- [[Ultrin]] asks if there is any magic that would solve the problem. [[Thameor Vex]] explains that no such spell exists within [[Neverwinter Academy]]. The spell that [[Nand]] cast was his own - which is documented, but only up to this point.
- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] thinks that moving [[Neverwinter Academy]] to restore the predictability of magic is a key first step, that must be taken before any attempt can be made at attempting to restore balance to [[The Weave]] and take on [[Nihal]]
	- [[Ultrin]] isnt convinced that this holds true, or how it would guarantee solving the problem.
	- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] explains that that knowledge of how to transport [[Neverwinter Academy]] is easy - the real problem would be where to move the [[Neverwinter Academy]] to. [[Ultrin]] suggests the river - either levitating or on some foundation. 
	- In order to move [[Neverwinter Academy]] would require the approval of the city.
	- [[Dean Maverin Soul]] points out that all powerful wizards are currently being employed in maintaining the barrier - if any are taken from this task then either the barrier fails or it shrinks.
- [[Professor History]] states that more data is required before any decision can be made. He requires all data. He states that the people of [[Neverwinter]] are more important than [[Neverwinter Academy]]. [[Ultrin]] agrees, except that there may be something of great value within [[Neverwinter Academy]] that could prove to be the critical piece in defeating [[Nihal]]
	- [[Professor History]] points out that taking the portable barrier outside the main barrier would give much data on how magic works. Experimentation is important. 
- [[Ultrin]] asks how long the current position of the barrier can be maintained. He’s told it’s uncertain and depends on the mages - it could be forever, or more realistically the barrier will shrink again (which will happen sooner with the [[Neverwinter Academy]] corridor).
- [[Ultrin]] states he is headed for the [[House of Knowledge]] and any who wish to go with him may do. He will conduct his research and ask [[Lord Neverember]] about moving [[Neverwinter Academy]]
- [[Thameor Vex]] waits until the others have left and tells [[Ultrin]] that [[Nand]] was not ”a by the book” wizard and was dabbling in things forbidden by the laws of [[Neverwinter Academy]]. He stresses that [[Nand]] saw something in [[Ultrin]] and trusted him - that if it was a tendency towards the forbidden then he should embrace this, as the elder evils do not have our morals.
	- [[Ultrin]] thanks him for his words, but says that [[Nand]] must have been something beyond all this, as he gave his life for this. He may have seen an even greater threat that he was already battling - or had perhaps seen the future.
- [[Ultrin]] goes looking for the portable barrier. All the researchers there step back and watch him. [[Ultrin]] feels as if the portable barrier is something akin to an artefact attended to him. 
	- [[Ultrin]] asks someone if its possible to carry to device without having it float behind him but is told that they don’t know how it works but they suggest he does what he did before.
	- [[Ultrin]] is informed that they have attempted to calibrate the device in a way such that if it collapses it should collapse safely. 
- [[Ultrin]] once again commands the device to follow him. He requests a crystal ball is fetched from storage for him. [[Ultrin]] follows the person headed to the armoury, making terrible small talk.
	- The armoury is filled with common quality magical items, various enchanted weapons and various enchanted pieces of ammunition.
	- Many of the shelves are empty
	- [[Ultrin]] finds a +2 staff and a quiver of 5 random magic arrows and a +1 short bow and 5 random potions
- [[Ultrin]] goes looking for [[Aust]], checking in the areas where plants were being grown 

## [[House of Knowledge]]

- [[Merric]] and [[Jorund]] follow a senior scribe down to the basement, passing different landings there are inscriptions on the stone “to preserve is to remember // to remember is to judge // to judge is to accept consequence”
- At the base of the staircase is presumably the [[Great Vault]]. There are many scribes working within the vault, sealing things with black wax and redacting pages in a highly destructive manner. There are grand black iron doors, in front of them is a very tall, elderly balding man, wearing pristine white robes and looking exhausted and frantic. He nodes to the scribe and introduces himself as the [[Grand Scribe]] - he notes that as the heroes of [[Neverwinter]] he is mandated to help us
	- [[Jorund]] explains we need his help with knowledge and that we require in-depth knowledge. [[Jorund]] offers that we can trade our knowledge, but that we require an understanding of how this happened so we can stop it.
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] thinks it’s very important to know what happened, but not how, so that it cannot be reproduced. 
	- [[Jorund]] explains that funeral rights are being twisted to attack the gods. 
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] fears that reading the books would give the secret of how to once again put the world in danger. He states that wizards always want to read the books “for the right reasons”. 
- [[Grand Scribe]] explains that [[Ultrin]]’s defection and hunger for applying knowledge to magic is a concern.
	- [[Merric]] and [[Jorund]] argue that it would be inaction of the [[Grand Scribe]] that would lead to the death of all if [[Kyusse]] cannot be defeated. 
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] grants that the redaction can be halted, but that the vault must be sealed. The [[Grand Scribe]] confesses that only wizards would be a threat if they had this knowledge, but he is unsure if his scribes would be able to find the knowledge. 
	- [[Grand Scribe]] offers to grant us access to the index of the vault that would be relevant to our interests. [[Jorund]] explains that we need to understand the corrupted rights and how this is used to attack the afterlife. 
- The [[Grand Scribe]] thinks this could be linked to apotheosis - the living sacrifice that becomes something else as they die during the ritual. 
	- He explains there are books that describe the elements of apotheosis - a threshold that must be crossed, a sacrifice and someone that bears witness (ideally many).
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] explains that they need someone who is knowledge in funeral rights to help with this - [[Jorund]] points out we have an expert in the funeral rights of the [[Raven Queen]], and that these aren’t wizards!
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] is begrudgingly willing to grant access to the [[Raven Queen]] order ([[Merric]] points out this is only 3 people).
- [[Merric]] asks about the history of [[Kyusse]] and his link to [[Nihal]] and if there could be any value there. 
- The [[Grand Scribe]] is happy for [[Jorund]] to have access to the knowledge and is happy with the judgement of [[Jorund]] and [[Merric]]. He hands a tiny key to [[Jorund]] and stresses that this makes him a custodian to all knowledge considered dangerous enough to be sealed away. 
- [[Merric]] asks the [[Grand Scribe]] if he feels there could be any risk of the funeral of [[Nand]] becoming its own misused rite that could cause its own problems (like [[Nand]] becoming a lich?!)
	- There is a suggestion that a non-religious funeral is performed for [[Nand]] instead
- The [[Grand Scribe]] describes the way that [[Starspawn of Nihal|Starspawn]] are usually created. He explains it takes much worship to be able to create a singular star spawn and there aren’t enough people in the city to summon this many. Perhaps the [[Starspawn of Nihal]] are the ones worshipping [[Nihal]] and this has reached a tipping point?
	- [[Merric]] asks the [[Grand Scribe]] if he knows of any weaknesses. They are known to use psychic powers, so defend against those. 
- [[Merric]] informs the [[Grand Scribe]] of the situation with [[Zugtamoid]], the [[Araumycos]] and the [[Sharn]]. 
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] was unaware of being in multiple wars! He points out that the connection is the shared consciousness that each element has. 
	- Some of the myconids in the [[Underdark]] align to [[Zugtamoid]]
	- The [[Grand Scribe]] wonders who performed the marriage of [[Zugtamoid]] and the [[Araumycos]] and that these entities could be ones that are worshiping [[Nihal]] 
- [[Merric]] checks on whether [[Jarick]] has checked out any books recently - he hasn’t checked out anything for about a month. 
	- He was checking out books on herbs. His last book he checked out was part of an alphabetic run of books on draconic blood lines, ending with Gold.
