## In a glade
- The party (inc [[Merric]]) sees a glade phase into existence. The glade is  otherwise empty save for a white stag. Every moment there is more to see. There is a great oak standing in the glade, charred and still crackling with lightning. 
	- A river runs past the oak. In the river is a female in glowing robes, ethereal and ghost like, kneeling down in the stream. 
	- [[Silvanus]] (often seen as a stag) has 2 subservient deities - Malar (nature’s destructive forces - behind the oak?) and Eldath (goddess of tranquility - in the river?) 
	- Behind the oak is a large beast like humanoid
	- [[Merric]] doesn’t remember how he got here - walking through a strange forest, seeing a tree that was rotting and seeing some dog/s.
- A glowing white spirit of [[Aust’s Mother]] appears from behind the oak and approaches [[Aust]], her feet hovering above the floor of the glade, and her being disperses and coalesces into a glowing white ball.
	- [[Aust]] picks up the ball, it feels comfortably warm
	- [[Aust]] can feel that his soul is trying to pull itself towards both the tree and the river
- [[Aust]] stands and moves towards both the tree and the river.
	- [[Merric]] tries reaching into his magic for a weapon but can’t make it manifest.
	- The stag is watching [[Aust]] as he walks around it. [[Aust]] sees the other figures are also watching him.
	- [[Aust]] feels like there is some choice to be made on behalf of this mother’s soul - between a path of revenge or peacefulness. He moves towards the water, stepping into it and towards the figure there.
	- The avatar speaks to [[Aust]], but the others cannot hear. “The river does not rush to the sea, it waits at the bend and asks the stones which way to flow”. [[Aust]] understands that if he lays the spirit to rest in the river his mother would be at rest and become a source of healing.
	- The soul hovers above the water and then disperses into a thin mist and flows with the water until it blends and vanishes.
	- [[Merric]] and [[Ultrin]] notice a faint veil of mist appear around [[Aust]]’s shoulders. [[Merric]] also notices some small flowers blooming along the river banks around [[Aust]].
- The light of the glade begins to dull and once again we are stood on a skiff with [[The Ferryman]].

## On the boat
- The oar that [[The Ferryman]] is using now appears to be a staff of twisted roots. One of his eyes is glowing a faint green.
	- [[Aust]] asks about the oar and the eye. Gets no response.
	- [[Merric]] and [[Ultrin]] notice that the staff is some form of Druid focus, but there is something necromantic about the focus (but many things seem necromantic).
- [[Aust]] explains what just happened to [[Merric]]. [[Merric]] says he has amnesia. 
- [[Aust]] asks if anyone knows what the aura around him is. [[Ultrin]] thinks it is some magical aura that is now part of his being. He seems extra calm and peaceful, he seems younger and less stressed.
- [[Aust]] asks [[The Ferryman]] “where next green eyed man”. 
	- The man sighs and says “the river reveals, the river tells us everything but not all at once.“
- There is some conversation between the others about how religious they would need to be and whether they have done enough to have earned a trip to their afterlife
- [[Merric]] looks at [[The Ferryman]] and sees that he is deep in thought. [[Merric]] tries asking him telepathically what he is considering, gets a similar answer to [[Aust]] and is told that “the river reveals things for everyone”.
- The current of the river shifts and a warm breeze carrying the scent of smoke and barley picks up. In the distance a hill rises, atop it is a great tree, ringed with homes and lanterns. The river narrows and ends at the foot of the hill. Everyone gets out. 
	- [[Merric]] recognises the tree. He sighs and walks towards it saying we should get this over with
- The party walks over sun warmed grass. The river and boat has vanished behind them.
- There are lanterns swaying, hanging from the tree. Bees hum in the fields. The great tree is ringed with large carvings in the tribal dialect, carving their names into the tree as they come of age. There is a garland of leaves woven through the lowest boughs of the tree.
	- As they near the top of the hill, it feels familiar but different to [[Merric]]. Music is playing and there is laughter, but they are softer and more subdued than he remembers. A feast is being prepared - the kind that is prepared for an official occasion such as a death or news to be discussed. 
	- The whole clan has gathered, including [[Otho]], [[Verna]], [[Jungo]] and everyone else.
	- Walking around the crowd and heading to the tree, [[Merric]] realises that the [[Master of Locks]] is missing. [[Merric]] realises this is towards his last days at home - perhaps even the last day.
	- [[Merric]] spends some time climbing amongst the tree, looking for names of those he has lost. 
	- [[Aust]] joins him in climbing the tree. Thinks it’s not a good tree for climbing. 
	- [[Aust]] sees around them is a dense forest, scattered with other hills with similarly decorated trees, assumably with similar clans. They seem inconveniently far away, being far enough away. 
- [[Ultrin]] and [[Jorund]] head out into the feast. They are ignored by the other people at the feast. They realise that as they take things from the feast it replenishes itself. 
	- One member of the clan seems more popular than the rest, they seem to be the oldest person at the gathering, looks a bit frail. 
	- [[Merric]] sees this is [[Otho]]. [[Verna]] is moving around, helping with the feast. [[Jungo]] is off to one side, whittling away at something. [[Merric]] goes to talk to [[Jungo]] and sees he is whittling away at a wooden short sword. 
	- [[Jungo]] remarks that the wood appears to be fighting him and doesn’t feel right. He says he gets a bad feeling when he’s working on it, but he’s not sure what its trying to tell him.
	- [[Merric]] asks where the wood came from and [[Jungo]] points up at the tree. He explains that the tree provides for us, while the elders don’t want us to protect ourselves or explore, but we could take the tree with us. 
	- [[Jungo]] mentions that [[Otho]] is looking for him, as he and the [[Master of Locks]] are both late. 
- [[Merric]] wanders through the feast and finds [[Jorund]] eating an endlessly replacing bread roll. It’s very tasty but isn’t filling at all. 
	- [[Ultrin]] asks [[Merric]] where his parents are, [[Merric]] waves in the general direction and mentions where his house is. [[Merric]] tells [[Ultrin]] there are no magical tomes to steal. 
- [[Merric]] grabs a leg meat and wonders across to [[Verna]] to grab a beer. He downs a drink and then wonders across to [[Otho]]
	- The people around [[Otho]] depart. [[Merric]] sees that [[Otho]] looks frustrated. [[Otho]] thanks him for finally turning up, that its an important day. [[Otho]] talks about the needs of the clan being more important than the individual, as a way of asking [[Merric]] to go and fetch the [[Master of Locks]]
	- [[Merric]] notices that [[Otho]] seems quite horse but still commands a presence. [[Merric]] thanks [[Otho]] for his service and leaves the feast.
- [[Jorund]] is posted to watch for the Orcs coming from the east as [[Merric]] wants to watch over the feast. 
	- [[Merric]] begins to feel a pain from resisting his memory. There is a brief moment of a cold feeling on his back, as if lying on a cold stone floor. [[Merric]] can smell something like smoke/a candle extinguished.
- [[Aust]] finds the [[Master of Locks]] and introduces himself and that he is being requested for the ceremony. [[Aust]] accompanies him back (walking very slowly).
- [[Merric]] loses himself, watching the festivities of the feast. (Double Nat 1)
	- [[Jorund]] starts to smell smoke and finds himself in a sudden attack by attacking Orcs. 
	- The clan members start making sounds of bird song and the screams begin as horns are blown in warning. The clan has no plan for this, no armaments for this and is in no way prepared. 
- [[Merric]] runs to [[Jungo]], who hands him a short sword that he had made previously. [[Jungo]] charges the orcs and [[Merric]] charges in alongside him. 
	- The fight goes poorly for the village. It is violent, bloody and over quickly. 
	- [[Merric]] clashes with an orc, taking a beating from it and is left dazed and breathless; then is winded and can’t breathe. There is the sound of screams all around. 
- On opening his eyes, [[Merric]] seems a room made of dark stone. It’s cold, damp, feels ancient. There is incense burning in braziers, carved with halfling runes and a circle of mushrooms ringing the ceiling above.
	- [[Merric]] is lying on his back on an elevated cold slab. A damp cold cloth is pressing against his brown in a comforting way. It’s still hard to breathe. He feels old and frail, as if he is dying.
- For [[Aust]], [[Jorund]] and [[Ultrin]] the vision fades and they are all in a dark, dank stone room, wearing ceremonial robes. [[Jorund]] is wearing a fancier robe with runes on it. They are standing in a circle around an old halfling, who looks a bit like [[Merric]] and a bit like [[Otho]]. [[Jorund]] is mopping the brow of the halfling.
	- [[Jorund]] and [[Ultrin]] feel this is a continuation of [[Merric]]’s path. The place feels wrong. It feels like some sort of chapel. 
	- [[Jorund]] recognises this as a halfling burial rite, but is lacking the earth required for Soul Earth. Plus the person isn’t dead (yet) - making it feel like a corrupted version of the rite.
	- [[Jorund]] can feel this is a perversion of the will of the [[Raven Queen]] - but that their purpose is to reenact what happened to understand what was done. It disturbs him that the [[Raven Queen]] inquisition was not aware of this.
	- [[Ultrin]] has a fleeting thought that understanding a ritual to circumvent the [[Raven Queen]]’s power would be useful to take notes. 
	- [[Jorund]] finds he has instructions for the rite on some paper in his pocket. The hand writing is not his own and it seems close to insane.
