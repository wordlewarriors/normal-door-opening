## On an ~~airship~~ flying ship
- The ship is ascending, it is floating but unsteady
- [[The Ferryman]] has moved to the bow of the ship and continues to use his staff as an oar (ineffectively) and is holding a lantern in the other hand
	- The lantern projects an image directly in front of the ship. 
	- The river below seems to fade into a mist and disappear. There is light shining from the sides of the boat - shimmering silver. This seems to be some protective magic (unsure what from). All without a spell being cast!
- The boat tilts and returns to a level heading, travelling towards the circle of light ahead. The boat appears to be widening, as if by magic
- The boat pierces the circle of light, briefly dazzling everyone. There are caverns blooming around the boat, towering mushrooms rising like cathedrals and in the centre is a writing “brain” of mycellium - its surface twitching with dark green light
	- [[Merric]] notices that the brain is coiled in dark tendrils. The tendrils don’t look violent, more like obscene intimacy. They begin to tighten, as if choking the brain. These are likely to arms of [[Zugtamoid]].
	- [[Aust]] points out that the brain is the [[Araumycos]]. During his time with the [[Sharn]], [[Aust]] had found that [[Zugtamoid]] had overtaken the [[Araumycos]] and was trying to spread further.
- Spores drift in the air around us, clinging to any surface they settle on. 
	- [[Merric]] sees that there are darkened fungal threads climbing their way over the edges of the boat. Where they contact the wood of the deck, they are singed and recoil.
	- Everyone becomes poisoned 
	- [[Ultrin]] can see the greater truth that this isn’t simply the union of 2 beings in the [[Underdark]] - this is under *every forest*. It is powerful and terrifying.
- [[The Ferryman]] says “Thus she spreads, beneath root and stone alike. The land itself is her garden now.” He shakes his staff & lantern, which briefly projects another circle, but the edges become jagged.
	- [[Jorund]] feels like this is similar to glass shattering in slow motion. 
	- Peering over the edge, the boat has not only been widening and lengthening but also growing taller. Yet there is no way to get below deck.
- The boat is unsteady as it surges forward. 
	- [[Jorund]] struggles to hold himself up.
- The fungal cavern is left behind and shattered into hexagonal shards and is gone. We found ourselves in a dream made flesh. A colossal amorphous body covered in pink is drifting within a barrier, its surface shimmering like quicksilver and fighting back a black mold which is constricting the barrier surrounding it. [[Aust]] tells us this is the [[Sharn]]
	- After a moment the barrier shrinks and then flares out, pushing the tendrils back. This then repeats but slower. 
	- [[Jorund]] is freaked out by this. 
	- [[Ultrin]] and [[Merric]] can see that with each outward pulse there is also a mirroring pulse of magic from the boat, protecting itself and us. Cracks are spreading across the deck in hexagonal patterns and with each pulse there is a faint magical rumbling in our skulls. It doesn’t feel safe, or unsafe.
	- [[Merric]] realises this is very similar to what is happening with the [[Araumycos]]. While the speed that the [[Araumycos]] spreads at is terrifying, if the [[Sharn]] is taken, she could spread at any speed she wants. This would not be good. 
- The hexagonal cracks in the ship glow purple and seem to be holding the ship together. The protective runes begin to glow brighter.  Once again, the boat begins to expand to the size of a fairly large ship.
- The vision around the ship shatters and for a moment the ship hangs in the void. Suddenly the ship appears to be sailing through the [[Sharn]]. The barrier collapses and the black fungus strikes. At the breach stands [[Grageon]] - his armour tattered, his skin burned with acid, wielding a trident, standing in desperate defiance. 
	- In the darkness behind [[Grageon]] is a gaunt figure chanting in a cracked quiet voice - each word that he speaks glues the barrier back together. This seems to be a warlock communing with his patron (which appears to be the multiverse himself) - [[Mr Johnson]]
	- A mist spreads over the deck from whatever [[Mr Johnson]] is working, and an acid like substance from [[Grageon]] both wash over the ship. Both of them appear to be slowly dying.
	- [[Jorund]] gets the feeling that each projection has been becoming more recent - this one appears to be currently happening.
- [[The Ferryman]] speaks. “They fight within the dream. When they fall, the [[Sharn]] falls, and when it falls, SHE will walk everywhere”
- The purple glow of the cracks in the deck flare with the divine radiance every time [[Grageon]] strikes. The boat is absorbing more and more power. 
- The vision tears apart and the boat is hurled into a vast void. In the distance are dead suns and vast stars. This is no kind of mortal sky.
- [[Ultrin]] feels small and out of place.
	- [[Merric]] is certain we have traveled to [[The Farm Realm]] - a place of dead gods where the elder evils dwell.
	- Directly ahead a star is stirring. It was a dead star, but is being reborn. The surface writhes with rivers of worms. It is a growing mass of endless hunger. With each pulse of the star it grows in size and power. It is tugging at our very souls, ravenously trying to consume. 
- The lantern on [[The Ferryman]]’s staff shatters and he drops his hood. His face is pale and his eyes fever bright. His rows are billowing and covered in arcane sigils. “I remember now, the spell, the planes!” It is [[Nand|Archmage Nandntiersanon]].
	- In a flash he is in front of [[Ultrin]]. Looking around him, all else seems to be frozen in time. This almost feels like home! His hand trembles as it rests on his shoulder
	- “[[Ultrin]], I have carried this spell as far as I can. I am merely its vessel, you are its heir. The planes are aligned but only you can finish this. Come with me, stand at the bow, see what the gods forbid us to see”
	- [[Ultrin]] thinks that he has been journeying through the visions of the different planes the party members are closest to, casting [[Nand]]’s spell the align the magic of all of the planes by passing through them. He thinks back to the forbidden ritual that [[Nand]] was channeling and the invitation to surrender their souls to the spell as [[Nand]] fired a shot through all of the planes towards [[Nihal]].
	- [[Ultrin]] thinks he understands what has and is happening, but has no clue what to do about it. 
- [[Ultrin]] joins [[Nand]] at the front of the boat. 
	- There are threads of silvery light from the boat, coiling around and meeting at the front of the boat. [[Ultrin]] thinks that this is [[the Weave]]. Beneath the boat, the planes stretch out in perfect alignment. You can see all the way through them down to [[Aust]]’s home. 
	- The silver threads begin to blacken as rot begins to rise through the planes as everything fills with mycelium and everything else is overwhelmed with toxic spore clouds. 
	- Everything becomes overwhelmed in decay and laid waste, temples fallen, the tombs of [[Oghma]] lost, the bastions of the [[Raven Queen]] fallen. All the religions have been corrupted by [[Nihal]] and power it instead
	- A solar flare of power erupts from [[Nihal]]. With every pulse [[the Weave]] is pulled into it. He sees a future where magic unravels, gods fade and magic is consumed and fades. All that is left is rot and worms. This is what happens if we fail.
	- [[Nand]] says more urgency “This is what lies ahead if you falter. She rots the prime, he consumes the weave. The gods themselves will be unmade, but you may yet change the tale.”
	- The silvery threads of [[the Weave]] blaze with light, fighting the blackness. They are infinite yet fragile and are being fully consumed. The strands coalesce into a silvery rotating sphere at the ship’s bow, within touching distance. 
	- “The weaves’ power is yours to command. The burden of ages rests upon your shoulders. But you cannot succeed alone.” 
	- [[Nand]] seems half proud of [[Ultrin]] and half afraid. “Quickly now, to your allies, return to me when you are prepared.”
	- Yet [[Ultrin]] hears another voice. “Why waste it. The weave is open. Vulnerable to you, hidden to everyone else. It would make a phylactery of unrivalled power. You could seal yourself eternal. All great ascendancy requires an audience. Leave them to wither.”
	- [[Ultrin]] is overwhelmed by the sense of concentrating on this entire place. It’s beyond his comprehension but he knows that it is true. The ship is some sort of weapon. All he has to do is somehow channel the weave into it - just has no clue how. But is urgent and the fate of everything depends on it. 
		- Somewhere at the back of his mind is a false memory of the ritual to bind [[the Weave]] to himself. [[The Weave]] is submitting to his will - he could ascend to godhood instead of firing the weapon - all he need do is convince the others.
