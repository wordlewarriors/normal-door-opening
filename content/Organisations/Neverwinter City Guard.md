## Overview

The Neverwinter City Guard is the primary military and law enforcement force protecting [[Neverwinter]] from external and internal threats. Led by Captain [[Mayer]], the organization is stationed strategically around the barrier perimeter that shields the city from the incursions of the [[Starspawn]]. The Guard represents one of the city's most visible institutions, serving as both defenders against extraplanar threats and maintainers of civic order.

## Current Status & Challenges

The Guard has been severely depleted by sustained combat against the [[Starspawn]] and previous encounters with hostile forces. The organization operates in a state of constant strain, with personnel stretched impossibly thin across multiple critical responsibilities. Beyond the exhausting perimeter defense duties, the Guard must simultaneously manage persistent crime throughout the city and investigate activities related to various cults operating within [[Neverwinter]]'s borders. This combination of demands—external defense, law enforcement, and counterintelligence operations—has left the organization significantly undermanned and overextended.

## Goals & Structure

The Guard's primary objective remains the integrity of the barrier protecting the city, with particular focus on detecting any signs of shrinkage that might indicate weakening defenses. Under Captain [[Mayer]]'s command, the force maintains constant vigilance at the perimeter while attempting to manage internal security concerns. The organizational structure reflects these dual priorities, though resources have been allocated overwhelmingly toward barrier defense given the existential threat posed by the [[Starspawn]].
