## Overview

The Raven Queen clergy is a religious order devoted to [[Raven Queen]], the goddess of death, transitions, and lost souls. Operating under the principle that death is a natural and necessary part of existence, the order works to ensure souls pass safely into the afterlife and are not trapped or corrupted in the mortal realm. The clergy maintains a presence in [[Neverwinter]] and surrounding areas, performing sacred rites and maintaining consecrated ground for the dead.

## Goals & Structure

The primary mission of the Raven Queen clergy is to shepherd lost souls to their proper rest and protect the sanctity of death from profane corruption. They serve as guardians against necromancy and the misuse of death magic that defies the natural order. The order is hierarchically structured with [[Sister Maelin Thorn]] serving as the local leader in [[Neverwinter]]. Members perform rituals of passage, consecrate burial grounds, and intervene when death rites are corrupted or abused.

## Current Crisis

The Raven Queen clergy faces significant threats to their mission. The goddess herself is under direct attack by an entity or force known as [[Nihal]], suggesting coordinated opposition against death magic and divine authority in the region. Additionally, the clergy has discovered that corrupted rites have been performed in the goddess's name—acts which the Raven Queen herself did not sanction or enable. This distinction is crucial: the corruption stems from external malice rather than divine negligence, placing the order in the difficult position of defending both their goddess's honor and their spiritual authority while addressing widespread desecration.
