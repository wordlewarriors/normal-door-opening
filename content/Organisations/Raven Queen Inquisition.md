## Overview

The Raven Queen Inquisition is an enforcement body that serves the [[Raven Queen]], operating as her instrument of authority and control. The organization appears to conduct investigations, recruitment, and pursuit of those connected to the Raven Queen's interests or perceived transgressions. They are organized with a hierarchical structure that includes at least the rank of High Inquisitor, suggesting a formal institutional framework with established protocols and authority chains.

## Goals & Structure

The Inquisition functions as both an investigative and enforcement organization, tasked with identifying and dealing with those who pose threats to or fail to meet the Raven Queen's expectations. They appear capable of distinguishing between willing cultists and those merely hired for services, indicating a sophisticated understanding of motivations and allegiances. The organization can pursue targets across regions and has the authority to recruit individuals, suggesting they offer opportunities for employment or advancement to those they deem valuable. Their hierarchical structure includes High Inquisitor positions, implying multiple ranks and administrative tiers.
