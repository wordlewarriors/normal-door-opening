## Overview

The Weave is the fundamental magical structure underlying all of reality, manifesting as silvery threads of light that coalesce into a rotating sphere. It serves as the source and conduit for all magical power in the world, connecting mortals, gods, and the fabric of existence itself. The Weave was traditionally maintained by [[Mystra]], goddess of magic, though her influence has been dramatically compromised.

The Weave's appearance is ethereal and otherworldly—threads of luminous energy forming intricate patterns that concentrate into a sphere of immense power. When intact, it represents perfect balance and stability. However, the Weave has become increasingly vulnerable and volatile due to recent catastrophic events.

## Corruption and Destabilization

[[Nihal]]'s solar flare pulses are actively consuming the Weave, threatening to unravel all magical structure and cause the gods themselves to fade from existence. This consumption has rendered the Weave vulnerable to external manipulation and has fundamentally weakened magic throughout reality.

Following [[Nand]]'s ritual sacrifice during the events at [[Neverwinter]], the Weave was catastrophically altered. An incomprehensible magical force was channeled through Nand's death and concentrated upon [[Ultrin]], transforming the fundamental nature of magic itself. The threads of the Weave became temporarily tangible, feeding into a sphere of energy that enveloped Ultrin like a shawl, granting him infinite magical power.

The consequences have been severe: magic throughout the world is now significantly less powerful than before, with spells and enchantments fundamentally changed. The tunnel to [[Neverwinter Academy]] experiences particularly volatile magical instability, making the Weave unpredictable and dangerous to those who channel it.

## Ultrin's Connection

Remnants of the Weave condensed permanently into [[Ultrin]] during Nand's ritual, creating an incomplete but powerful connection to magical reality itself. Ultrin now feels infinite power at his fingertips through this bond, though he perceives the connection as unfinished—suggesting further transformation or danger may yet unfold.
