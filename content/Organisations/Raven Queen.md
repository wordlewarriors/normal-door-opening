## Overview

The Raven Queen is a goddess of death, fate, and the passage of souls in the [[Neverwinter]] region. She maintains a prominent temple in the city that serves as both a spiritual center and a practical hub for managing funerary rites and investigating supernatural crises. The Raven Queen's clergy, known as keepers of passage, traditionally facilitate the transition of souls from the mortal realm. Her symbol is one of divine authority and connection to greater powers—[[Jorund]] carries this mark as evidence of his bond with her. The goddess is depicted as a figure of unfathomable darkness, with eyes that seem endless and ancient, embodying the mystery of death itself.

## Goals & Structure

The Raven Queen's primary divine function is maintaining the natural order of death and the passage of souls. Her clergy structure includes a [[High Inquisitor]] who oversees temple operations and investigations into supernatural disturbances. The organization includes keepers of passage like [[Kieran]], who perform sacred funerary rites and manage the spiritual health of the city. The Raven Queen appears reluctant about direct divine intervention in mortal fate, yet she has shown willingness to act when balance itself is threatened. She maintains strategic positions within [[Neverwinter]], with approximately ten clergy members positioned to offer aid during crises. Her broader divine interests include restoring balance between warring gods and addressing fundamental threats to the natural order.
