## Overview

The Neverwinter Guard serves as the primary military and law enforcement force of [[Neverwinter]]. They maintain order within the city, defend its walls, and enforce local laws. As an organized institution, they represent the city's monopoly on legitimate force and maintain a significant presence throughout [[Neverwinter]]'s various districts and neighborhoods.

## Goals & Structure

The Guard's primary objectives include protecting [[Neverwinter]]'s citizens, maintaining internal peace, and defending against external threats. The organization maintains a hierarchical military structure with officers commanding various units and patrols throughout the city.

A notable aspect of their operations involves their supply chain management. The Guard has secured a lucrative contract with the [[Leonard Family|Leonard family]] for all non-magical supplies and equipment. This arrangement pays the [[Leonard Family|Leonard family]] substantially above market rates, suggesting either significant profiteering or a deliberately inflated contract arrangement. Meanwhile, the Guard sources all magical items and enchanted equipment exclusively through the [[Neverwinter Academy]], giving the Academy considerable influence over the Guard's magical capabilities and resources.

### Overview

The Neverwinter City Guard is the primary military and law enforcement force protecting [[Neverwinter]] from external and internal threats. Led by Captain [[Mayer]], the organization is stationed strategically around the barrier perimeter that shields the city from the incursions of the [[Starspawn of Nihal|Starspawn]]. The Guard represents one of the city's most visible institutions, serving as both defenders against extraplanar threats and maintainers of civic order.

### Current Status & Challenges

The Guard has been severely depleted by sustained combat against the [[Starspawn of Nihal|Starspawn]] and previous encounters with hostile forces. The organization operates in a state of constant strain, with personnel stretched impossibly thin across multiple critical responsibilities. Beyond the exhausting perimeter defense duties, the Guard must simultaneously manage persistent crime throughout the city and investigate activities related to various cults operating within [[Neverwinter]]'s borders. This combination of demands—external defense, law enforcement, and counterintelligence operations—has left the organization significantly undermanned and overextended.

### Goals & Structure

The Guard's primary objective remains the integrity of the barrier protecting the city, with particular focus on detecting any signs of shrinkage that might indicate weakening defenses. Under Captain [[Mayer]]'s command, the force maintains constant vigilance at the perimeter while attempting to manage internal security concerns. The organizational structure reflects these dual priorities, though resources have been allocated overwhelmingly toward barrier defense given the existential threat posed by the [[Starspawn of Nihal|Starspawn]].
