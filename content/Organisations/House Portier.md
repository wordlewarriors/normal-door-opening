## Overview

House Portier is one of the four great noble houses that comprises the ruling council of [[Baldur's Gate]]. As a house of significant political influence, they maintain considerable wealth and standing within the city's aristocratic hierarchy. The house is identifiable by distinctive signet rings worn by its members.

## Campaign Involvement

A young noble of House Portier became an unwitting pawn in a dark ritual when they were used as a sacrifice in an [[Orcus]] reanimation ceremony. The use of a house member—identifiable by their signet ring—suggests the ritual required someone of noble or significant social standing. This event has profound implications for the house's political position and their relationship with other council members, as it directly targeted one of their own during what appears to be a coordinated supernatural threat against [[Baldur's Gate]].
