## Overview

The Council of 11 is a covert intelligence organization operating across multiple major cities. The council maintains a rigid structure of exactly eleven members, each stationed in a different city to maximize their geographic reach and information gathering capabilities. They function as a network of watchers and reporters, monitoring significant activities and developments across their respective regions. The organization operates in shadows, with most people unaware of their existence or influence.

Members of the Council of 11 maintain contact through spell-scars, a mystical communication method that allows them to relay information across vast distances. This communication breakdown represents a serious vulnerability for an organization that depends on reliable, secretive information sharing to function effectively. ## Goals & Structure

The Council operates with a decentralized structure, with each of the eleven members responsible for intelligence gathering in their assigned city. Their primary goal appears to be comprehensive surveillance and reporting on activities of interest, though the exact nature of their agenda and whom they ultimately report to remains obscure.
