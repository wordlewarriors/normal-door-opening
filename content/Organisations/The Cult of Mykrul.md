## Overview

The Cult of Mykrul is a profane religious organization devoted to [[Mykrul]], the deity of death and the dead. The cult has taken control of a former church, converting it into a site of dark worship. Their theology centers on the liberation of death itself—the belief that death should be "unbound" from its natural constraints. They venerate the concept of resurrection and the emergence of corpses from their graves, viewing death not as an end but as a transition to be manipulated and exploited for their purposes.

## Goals & Structure

The cult's primary objective is to raise the dead as an undead army, seeking to harness supernatural power beneath grave sites through dark rituals and profane ceremonies. They view death as a tool to be wielded rather than respected, and their ultimate goal appears to be amassing military power through necromantic means. The organization operates from their converted church, which serves as both a religious center and a location for conducting their dark rituals. The cult likely maintains a hierarchical structure typical of religious organizations, with senior members leading rituals and directing the group's necromantic activities.

## Campaign Involvement

The cult's presence in the [[Neverwinter]] region represents a significant threat, as their ambitions to create an undead army could destabilize the area.
