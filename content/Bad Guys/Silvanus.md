## Overview

[[Silvanus]] is the greater deity of nature, wild forests, and natural cycles in the Forgotten Realms. He is typically depicted as a magnificent white stag, embodying the untamed wilderness in its most majestic form. As a powerful nature god, Silvanus maintains dominion over forests, trees, and the primal forces of the natural world. He commands two significant subservient deities who represent different aspects of nature: [[Malar]], representing nature's destructive and predatory forces, and [[Eldath]], goddess of peace, tranquility, and healing waters.

## Threat & Motivations

Silvanus maintains an enigmatic presence in the campaign, appearing not as a direct antagonist but as an observer and judge of mortal actions. His primary interest seems centered on natural balance and the spiritual integrity of those who commune with or affect the wild. Rather than seeking to cause harm, Silvanus appears motivated by ensuring that the natural order remains intact and that mortals who interact with the divine demonstrate worthy intentions and understanding of nature's true complexity.
