## Overview

[[Kyransalee]] is a deity of undeath and necromancy who commands a devoted following among certain drow circles, particularly [[House T'sarran]]. Her influence extends to the surface world, where she has been connected to the devastating undead plague that afflicted [[Neverwinter]]. Her worship combines elements of dark magic, soul binding, and the corruption of death itself into a tool of domination.

## Threat & Motivations

[[Kyransalee]]'s primary threat manifests through her corrupting influence over death and undeath. She seeks to expand her dominion by binding souls to her service through arcane rituals and religious devotion. The undead plague affecting [[Neverwinter]] appears directly connected to her worship, suggesting either active intervention or the machinations of her most devoted followers. Her ability to grant power to chosen servants makes her a significant supernatural threat, particularly given the demonstrated competence of her previous agent who the party defeated.

## Key Events

The party encountered a chosen of [[Kyransalee]], whom they successfully killed. This chosen's death became significant enough to serve as a passphrase for identifying allies—specifically recognized by [[Nym]] as proof of trustworthiness. Later investigation revealed extensive religious materials dedicated to [[Kyransalee]] in [[Ivaran Sylhorn]]'s private library, protected by magical wards, indicating deep scholarly or ritualistic interest. A grotesque trophy—a preserved drow head bearing her sacred symbols—was discovered in the same location, with "betrayer" carved into the skin, suggesting ritualistic punishment of apostates or enemies.

[[Kyransalee]] maintains strong ties to [[House T'sarran]] and their drow operations. [[Ivaran Sylhorn]] appears to be a significant devotee or scholar of her teachings.
