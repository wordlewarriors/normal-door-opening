## Overview

Dorisane is the King of the Ghouls and an Exarch of [[Orcus]], making him a formidable power within the supernatural hierarchy. As an Exarch, he serves the demon prince directly and commands considerable influence over undead and ghoulish forces. His authority extends throughout the ghoul kingdoms, where he is recognized as a major threat that demands respect from both allies and enemies alike.

## Threat & Motivations

Dorisane's primary power base lies in his command over ghouls and other undead servants. As an agent of [[Orcus]], his motivations align with the demon prince's dominion over death and undeath. He appears to be building or maintaining a faction with defined boundaries—notably, he tolerates ghouls within his hierarchy but explicitly does not tolerate demons, suggesting he either limits demonic influence in his territory or maintains strict control over which infernal powers operate within his domain. This distinction indicates he may be consolidating power independently while still honoring his obligations to Orcus.

## Connections

Dorisane is served by [[Duanaogar the Defier]], a loyal subordinate who helps execute his will. His position as an Exarch places him within the broader supernatural network, though his preference for ghoul allies over demons suggests he maintains some autonomy in selecting his inner circle. His relationship with [[Orcus]] is significant but may not be entirely restrictive, given his apparent authority to exclude demonic forces from his faction.
