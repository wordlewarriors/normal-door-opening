## Overview

[[Tarrith]] is a drow mercenary currently imprisoned at the [[Halls of Justice]]. He is distinguished by being part of an identical twin pair, with his brother being [[Nym]], who is also employed as a mercenary. [[Tarrith]] was captured and incarcerated following the arson attack on [[My Chemical Reagents]].

## Threat & Motivations

[[Tarrith]] was hired to burn down [[My Chemical Reagents]], claiming the contract came through dead drops bearing [[Lord Neverember]]'s seal. His primary motivation appears to be protecting his twin brother [[Nym]], suggesting his mercenary work may be driven by familial obligation or coercion rather than pure greed. As a skilled operative willing to commit arson for hire, he represents a direct physical threat to [[Neverwinter]]'s infrastructure and citizens.

## Key Events

[[Tarrith]] was previously held as a prisoner at [[Neverwinter Manor]], where he attempted to escape. Despite [[Edgar]]'s efforts to prevent the escape and resistance to bribes offered to facilitate it, [[Tarrith]] eventually succeeded in breaking free. He subsequently carried out the arson attack on [[My Chemical Reagents]], leading to his recapture and current imprisonment at the [[Halls of Justice]].

## Connections

[[Tarrith]] has knowledge of drow religious affiliations, specifically recognizing members of [[House T'sarran]] as worshippers of [[Kyransalee]]. His employment through sealed dead drops suggests connections to high-ranking officials or those capable of forging official documents. His bond with his identical twin brother [[Nym]] represents a significant leverage point.
