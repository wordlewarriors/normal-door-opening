## Overview

Vecna is a powerful and ancient evil entity with a deeply disturbing obsession with forbidden knowledge and control. The entity is known to create leather-bound books—grimoires of exceptional malevolence—bound in human flesh, particularly using the faces of victims for the most powerful volumes. These bound tomes serve as vessels for dark arcane power and represent Vecna's twisted philosophy that knowledge and flesh are interchangeable commodities in the pursuit of ultimate authority.

## Threat & Motivations

Vecna's primary motivation appears to be the accumulation and control of knowledge through any means necessary, with a secondary drive to extend influence across vast distances. The creation of flesh-bound books serves dual purposes: each volume acts as a scrying focus, allowing Vecna to sense and observe whoever opens or reads from it. Additionally, these grimoires can transmit Vecna's will directly to readers through magical inscriptions, effectively making them vessels for remote instruction and control. This suggests a long-term strategy to spread influence through artifacts that compromise anyone who engages with them. The use of human faces as binding material indicates either deep cruelty or a ritualistic belief system linking mortality, identity, and power.

## Connections

The presence of Vecna's work in the [[Neverwinter]] region during this arc suggests either active operations in the area or the circulation of his creations through trade networks, cults, or corrupted agents. The exact nature of how these books entered the campaign's awareness remains unclear, but their existence poses an immediate threat to any who might carelessly open such volumes.
