## Overview

The Master is an eldritch entity of incomprehensible alien nature, summoned into the material plane by [[the Elf]] through the [[sphere of annihilation]]. It does not belong to this reality and its very presence warps the fundamental laws of existence around it. The entity manifests in multiple forms: initially as a sphere of pure nothingness containing a single luminous watching eye at its center, and when fully manifested, transforms into a cataclysmic shower of countless crimson eyes that bloom and multiply across the sky like a meteor storm. Each eye appears sentient and aware, creating an overwhelming sensation of being watched from all angles simultaneously.

The entity appears to acknowledge [[the Elf]] specifically—all manifestations focus their countless eyes upon its summoner in recognition—suggesting the Elf may have some form of connection or control over it, though this relationship remains unclear. Whether the Master acts with purpose and intelligence or follows blind cosmic imperatives remains unknown, but its appearance represents an extinction-level threat to the surrounding area and potentially beyond.
