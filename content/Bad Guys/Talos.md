## Overview

[[Talos]] is a god of destruction, storms, and chaos. His followers operate under the guise of legitimate weather practitioners and magicians, using this disguise to infiltrate communities and gain access to vulnerable populations. The priesthood employs a deliberate strategy of deception, presenting themselves as helpful weather workers while secretly conducting rituals to invoke catastrophic natural disasters.

## Threat & Motivations

The priests of [[Talos]] seek to spread chaos and devastation across the lands. Their primary motivation is to manifest the destructive power of their god through extreme weather events—torrential storms, earthquakes, droughts, and other uncontrolled natural calamities. They view destruction not as a consequence but as a sacred duty, believing that chaos purges the world and reveals hidden truths. By posing as weather magicians, they gain the trust of communities before orchestrating disasters that kill indiscriminately and leave ruin in their wake. Their ultimate goal appears to be widespread devastation that honors their deity and advances their apocalyptic vision.

## Key Events

The infiltration of the [[Neverwinter]] region by [[Talos]]'s priests marks a significant threat to local populations. These false weather workers have begun operating openly, leveraging their disguise to conduct rituals and invoke dangerous storms. Communities that initially believed they were hiring legitimate practitioners for weather control have become victims of orchestrated catastrophes, suggesting a coordinated campaign by the cult to establish a stronghold in the area.
