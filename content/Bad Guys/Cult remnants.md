## Overview

Scattered remnants of various cults that once operated in [[Neverwinter]] continue to maintain an active presence throughout the city. Rather than a single unified organization, these are multiple fractured groups pursuing their own agendas while collectively destabilizing the region. Their decentralized nature makes them difficult to track and eliminate, as suppressing one cell does little to disrupt the broader criminal ecosystem they've created.

## Threat & Motivations

These cult remnants engage in organized crime and deliberate acts of chaos across [[Neverwinter]]. Their motivations vary by faction—some seek to rebuild their former power and influence, others pursue specific ideological goals, and many are simply motivated by profit and survival. Their combined criminal activities include trafficking, extortion, sabotage, and recruitment of new members from desperate populations.

## Key Events

The cult remnants have escalated their operations to the point where they are stretching city resources dangerously thin. [[Arlon]] and the city guard have been forced into a defensive posture, responding to incidents across multiple districts simultaneously rather than mounting coordinated suppression campaigns. This reactive stance suggests the remnants are either growing bolder or better organized than previously understood, or possibly that their numbers have increased beyond initial estimates.

Their activities directly impact [[Arlon]]'s ability to maintain order in [[Neverwinter]], creating a strategic challenge requiring either additional military resources, political solutions, or targeted intelligence operations against key leadership figures within individual cells.
