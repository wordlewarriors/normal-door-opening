## Overview

[[Kiaransalee]] is a deity of undeath and necromancy who commands a devoted following among certain drow circles, particularly [[House T'sarran]]. Her influence extends to the surface world, where she has been connected to the devastating undead plague that afflicted [[Neverwinter]]. Her worship combines elements of dark magic, soul binding, and the corruption of death itself into a tool of domination.

## Threat & Motivations

[[Kiaransalee]]'s primary threat manifests through her corrupting influence over death and undeath. She seeks to expand her dominion by binding souls to her service through arcane rituals and religious devotion. The undead plague affecting [[Neverwinter]] appears directly connected to her worship, suggesting either active intervention or the machinations of her most devoted followers. Her ability to grant power to chosen servants makes her a significant supernatural threat, particularly given the demonstrated competence of her previous agent who the party defeated.
