He is defined by his fervent commitment to dark religious practices and his wielding of a ritualistic dagger constructed from an unusual dark material that absorbs light rather than reflecting it. His physical scarring suggests a history of either self-mortification in service to his faith or violent encounters with those who oppose his worship.
