## Overview

[[Syrric]] is a deity of trickery, deception, and discord who actively works to corrupt and destroy organizations from within. Represented by the symbol of a white jawless skull radiating across a purple sunburst, [[Syrric]] embodies the principle that the most effective destruction comes not from external assault, but from internal corruption and ideological subversion. This god operates through subtle manipulation and the exploitation of existing fault lines within communities and institutions.

## Threat & Motivations

[[Syrric]]'s primary threat lies in his methodology: rather than direct confrontation, his followers embed tainted ideologies into established sects and organizations, quietly warping their foundational beliefs and turning members against one another. This approach is far more insidious than open warfare, as victims often fail to recognize the source of their fractioning until irreparable damage has been done.

The god's ultimate motivation appears to be the sowing of chaos through division. By transforming genuine faith communities and tight-knit groups into festering grounds of suspicion, betrayal, and ideological conflict, [[Syrric]] achieves wholesale destruction without requiring direct divine intervention. His followers act as agents of slow corruption, poisoning institutions from the inside out and leaving behind nothing but ruins and broken relationships.
