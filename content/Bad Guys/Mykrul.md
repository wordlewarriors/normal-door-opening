## Overview

[[Mykrul]] was once a mortal who ascended through undeath to godhood, rising alongside [[Bane]] and [[Bhaal]] in the pantheon. His core doctrine centered on a radical philosophy: that death is inevitable and absolute, and that even divine beings are subject to mortality. Though Mykrul himself has since perished, his influence persists through devoted cults that continue to draw upon the residual power embedded in his legacy. His followers interpret his death not as a failure but as the ultimate validation of his teachings—proof that nothing, not even a god, can escape death's dominion. These cultists have transformed that grim certainty into a theology of their own, venerating death as the supreme force and seeking to accelerate its work in the world.
