## Overview

Shadow Urns are three magical vessels encountered during the campaign in [[Neverwinter]]. These dark artifacts serve as conduits for shadow magic, emitting potent waves of darkness that ripple outward in a 10-foot radius. The urns are bound to an [[Elf]] adversary and function as part of a larger ritual or magical network designed to strengthen their defenses and bolster their allies. Each urn contains a viscous, shadowy dark blood that pulses with arcane energy. The three vessels appear to operate in synchronization, their power fluctuating in rhythm with a deep, resonant heartbeat sound.

## Abilities & Behaviour

The Shadow Urns actively project waves of shadow magic that serve multiple functions within their network. Their primary effect is to generate and maintain a protective shield around their associated [[Elf]], making them formidable defensive artifacts. Additionally, the shadow waves empower nearby [[Drow]] warriors, enhancing their combat capabilities and resolve. The urns synchronize their magical pulses in concert with a rhythmic heartbeat, suggesting they operate as a unified magical system rather than independent objects. When the urns are destroyed, they leak shadow in all directions, creating a chaotic release of dark energy. However, the destruction of the urns, while causing significant magical disruption, does not prevent the [[Elf]]'s ultimate plans from proceeding forward.
