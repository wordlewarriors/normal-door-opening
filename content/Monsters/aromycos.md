## Overview

Aromycos is a sentient fungal entity of immense power that has become the driving force behind a catastrophic plague afflicting [[Neverwinter]]. Once a natural force of decomposition and renewal, it has been corrupted—a perversion of its original purpose. The entity manifests through vast networks of mushrooms and mycelial growth that spread disease and death across the land. Its corruption appears deliberate and malicious, suggesting external influence or manipulation, though the exact nature of this corruption remains unclear.

## Abilities & Behaviour

Aromycos communicates through its fungal manifestations, particularly through mushrooms that have developed an unsettling capacity for speech. These mushrooms obsessively repeat the cryptic phrase "She is wed, she is beautiful," suggesting a connection to some female entity or event. This repetition may indicate either Aromycos's corrupted thoughts or compulsion, or it may be a message being forced through the entity against its will.

The fungus spreads plague through spores and mycelial networks, capable of infecting vast areas and populations. Its influence extends through the environment itself, corrupting natural growth and turning it into a vector for disease. The scale of its power is evident in the catastrophic plague it has unleashed, affecting multiple regions and threatening entire settlements.

## Encounters

The [[Archdruid]] has expressed uncertainty about how to restore Aromycos to its natural state, implying the entity may be saveable rather than simply destroyed. This suggests that direct confrontation may not be the ideal solution, and that understanding the nature of its corruption—particularly the mysterious "She" referenced by the mushrooms—may be key to resolving this crisis.
