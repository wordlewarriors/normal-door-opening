## Overview

Araumycos is a vast fungal entity that exists beneath the forests of the world, a sprawling consciousness of mycelial networks and fungal matter. It functions as a form of natural intelligence, capable of communication through its writhing surface and subtle environmental changes. Once a powerful druidic force maintaining natural balance, Araumycos has become a critical battleground in a larger supernatural conflict.

The entity was first encountered as a distillate preserved in [[Ivaran Sylhorn]]'s kitchen—a concentrated, dangerous extract that warns against deep inhalation, suggesting the entity's physical form possesses toxic or transformative properties. This preserved sample hints at the entity's potent nature and the interest various parties have taken in understanding or weaponizing it.

## Corruption & Decline

Araumycos has been overtaken and is being systematically consumed by [[Zugtamoid]]'s dark tendrils through what druids describe as a "corrupted marriage." The pace of this consumption is terrifying, spreading across the fungal substrate at an accelerating rate. As Zugtamoid's corruption takes hold, it manifests as rot and fungal spores spreading through the air across [[Neverwinter]], poisoning the environment and forcing the druid circles into silence.

The druids, who once maintained a connection to Araumycos as a natural ally, have ceased their communications and withdrawn from active engagement, suggesting either direct suppression or profound despair at the entity's corruption. If Zugtamoid completes its consumption of Araumycos, the corrupted fungal brain would become a weapon of significant power.
