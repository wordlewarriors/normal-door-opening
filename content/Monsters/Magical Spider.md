## Overview

A formidable predator inhabiting the sewers beneath [[Ivaran Sylhorn]]'s manor, this spider represents an unusual fusion of natural creature and arcane power. Its most distinctive feature is a vivid blue glowing rune emblazoned across its thorax, suggesting either deliberate magical binding or an innate mystical nature. The rune pulses with eldritch energy, distinguishing it from ordinary arachnids and marking it as a genuine threat to adventurers exploring the underground passages beneath the manor. The origin of this magical enhancement remains unclear—whether it was deliberately placed by [[Ivaran Sylhorn]] or emerged naturally from the strange magical confluence of the sewers themselves.

## Abilities & Behaviour

The spider demonstrates remarkable hunting prowess through multiple dangerous capabilities. Its webbing serves as both a mobility tool and an offensive weapon, effectively entangling prey and restricting movement during combat. More insidiously, its saliva possesses corrosive properties potent enough to dissolve conventional armor and metal, making physical protection less reliable than usual. The creature exhibits heightened aggression and tenacity compared to natural spiders, pursuing targets relentlessly once combat is initiated rather than retreating to safety. Its relatively large size provides additional advantages in strength and reach, allowing it to dominate encounters in confined spaces like sewers. The purpose of the blue rune remains enigmatic—whether it enhances its physical abilities, enables spell casting, or serves some other function has yet to be determined.

## Encounters

When confronted in its sewer domain, the spider leverages environmental advantages effectively, using tight corridors and vertical surfaces for superior positioning.
