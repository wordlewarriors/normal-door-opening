## Overview

Janice is the head of the maps department at the [[House of Knowledge]] in [[Neverwinter]]. She is a meticulous and cautious custodian of cartographic resources, taking her responsibilities seriously regarding who gains access to sensitive materials.

## Role in the Campaign

Janice serves as a gatekeeper to valuable maps and geographical information that the party requires for their investigations. Her position grants her authority over what materials can be accessed and by whom, making her an important contact for acquiring specialized knowledge or navigation aids.

## Key Events

When the party first approached Janice seeking map access, [[Ultrin]] attempted to become invisible to bypass her authority.
