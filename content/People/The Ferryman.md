## Overview

The Ferryman is a spectral boatman who operates a skiff on the River Styx, ferrying souls across the boundary between life and death. He is revealed to be [[Archmage Nandntiersanon]] (called "Nand"), an ancient and powerful spellcaster who has undergone profound transformation through his role as a planar vessel. His appearance has evolved dramatically across encounters—from a skeletal, hooded figure with glowing red eyes to a more humanoid form dressed in middle-class robes, bearing physical markers of multiple planes: one eye reflecting Feywild green and the other showing Shadowfell shadow. His magical implement transformed from a simple oar into a staff of twisted roots, and the boat itself bears the craftsmanship signatures of [[Neverwinter Academy]].

## Role in the Campaign

The Ferryman serves as a guardian and guide through the liminal spaces between planes. He is tasked with warding off anomalies on the River Styx and enforcing its fundamental law: that gods have no power upon it. His primary mission extends far beyond simple ferrying—he is the vessel for an ancient planar alignment spell designed to harmonize magic across different planes. Through his journey down the river, Nand has gathered knowledge of fate manipulation and planar connections, positioning him as both a guide and a source of critical magical expertise in the [[Plague Investigation]] arc and beyond.

## Key Events

The party first encountered the Ferryman during their crossing of the River Styx, where he was surprised by their willingness to sacrifice together for passage. He recognized supernatural taint on [[Merric]] and [[Ultrin]]'s souls and warned that their journey would progress through all planes toward the [[Astral Sea]]. Over subsequent sessions, he became increasingly unstable—transfixed by disturbances in the water and panicking when the boat ran aground, suggesting his grip on reality was fragmenting. By Session 33, the full truth emerged: Nand transferred the burden of the planar alignment spell to [[Ultrin]], seeking his aid in completing it at the bow of a flying ship, half-proud and half-afraid of relinquishing his ancient responsibility.

## Relationships

[[Ultrin]] appears to be central to Nand's purpose, as the Ferryman specifically recognizes the taint upon his soul and ultimately entrusts him with the planar alignment spell. The party's initial encounter demonstrated mutual surprise—Nand was shocked by their unified sacrifice, suggesting he rarely witnesses such cooperation. His awareness of [[Merric]]'s tainted soul indicates he possesses deep insight into the party's spiritual conditions. Nand's transformation from guardian to supplicant suggests a complex relationship with his burden, seeking partnership rather than solitude in completing his cosmic mission.
