## Overview

Sister Maelin Thorn is the highest-ranking [[Raven Queen]] clergy member currently serving in [[Neverwinter]]. She holds the position of chief funerary officer for the Raven Queen's priesthood, giving her significant authority over mortuary rites, funeral practices, and the sacred spaces where the dead are honored. As a representative of the priesthood in civic matters, she commands both religious and political influence in the city.

## Role in the Campaign

Sister Maelin serves as a crucial intermediary between the adventuring party and the established religious institutions of Neverwinter. She was invited by [[Lord Neverember]] to attend his proclamation, representing the priesthood's interests in governmental matters. Her ecclesiastical authority makes her a key figure in matters involving desecration, corruption of sacred rites, and proper handling of the deceased—all matters that have directly affected the party's investigations and missions.

## Key Events

During the investigation into corrupted funerary practices, Sister Maelin confirmed that the [[Raven Queen]] did not willingly enable or sanction the corrupted rites that had been discovered. She identified approximately 2-3 cracked mausoleums in a rough area of [[Neverdeath Graveyard]] as locations of concern. She also made the practical decision to defer the funeral of [[Nand]] until after the party's planned mission to the graveyard, explaining that tolling funeral bells prevent spirits from dispersing to other planes—a consideration that affects both spiritual protocol and tactical timing.

## Relationships

Sister Maelin maintains a professional relationship with [[Lord Neverember]] and represents the Raven Queen's interests in his administration. She works closely with the party as a consultant on matters of desecration and funeral rites, demonstrating willingness to collaborate on investigations affecting her goddess's sacred spaces.
