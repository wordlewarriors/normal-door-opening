## Overview

Mr Johnson is a mysterious warlock whose patron is the multiverse itself. He possesses an otherworldly connection that grants him the ability to commune with cosmic forces far beyond typical arcane understanding. His nature remains largely enigmatic, with little revealed about his origins or personal history.

## Role in the Campaign

Mr Johnson serves as a crucial figure in the desperate struggle to contain the threat within [[the Sharn]]. Working in tandem with [[Grageon]], he uses his warlock abilities to repair and maintain the Sharn's barrier through ritualistic chanting. His role is instrumental but costly—both he and Grageon are slowly dying as they pour their life force into this containment effort, suggesting they are willing to sacrifice themselves for the greater good.

## Key Events

Mr Johnson was first encountered by [[Izz]] during a teleportation visit to [[Phandalin]], where he functioned as a contact, though his significance was not immediately apparent at the time. His most notable appearance occurred during the critical moment when he stood in darkness behind [[Grageon]], chanting in a cracked, quiet voice as he worked to repair the Sharn's barrier. This desperate ritual revealed the true scope of his power and commitment to containing whatever threat lurks within the Sharn.
