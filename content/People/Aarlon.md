#Neverwinter 

- A member of the [[Harpers]], based in [[Neverwinter]]. 
- [[Aarlon]] had a plan to assassinate [[Lord Neverember]] by attempting to poison him and replace him. This plan failed, due to the protection given to [[Lord Neverember]] by the [[White Sails]] mercenary company that [[Lord Neverember]] had brought into [[Neverwinter]].

## Overview

Aarlon is a spy and investigator operating within [[Neverwinter]]'s power structures. He maintains an unusual working relationship with [[Lord Neverember]], appearing as a junior partner in the [[Halls of Justice]]. Despite his official position, Aarlon operates primarily in the shadows, conducting surveillance and gathering intelligence on corruption within the city's leadership.

## Role in the Campaign

Aarlon serves as a valuable intelligence asset and occasional ally to the party. He was initially approached by the adventurers to help monitor [[My Chemical Reagents]] and has since become a recurring contact for information about political intrigue in [[Neverwinter]]. His position within the [[Halls of Justice]] allows him access to city resources and personnel, including the [[Greycloaks]] and [[Nashers]], which he has leveraged to assist in maintaining order during crises.

## Key Events

Aarlon was imprisoned following an assassination attempt but negotiated his release by accepting a 30-day Geas with the binding instruction to "Serve Neverwinter." This magical compulsion causes him physical pain when violated, creating a complicated constraint on his actions. He has been actively surveilling members of the [[Council of Neverwinter]] and uncovered evidence that a corrupt counselor hired the [[Tarrith]] to monitor [[Lord Neverember]]. During the plague investigation, Aarlon coordinated emergency resupply efforts for armed forces and revealed that a [[Drow]] was responsible for burning down [[My Chemical Reagents]], while also disclosing that [[Nym]], brother of [[Tarrith]], was staying at the [[Beach Leviathan]].

## Relationships

[[Aarlon]] maintains a complex dynamic with [[Lord Neverember]], serving as both subordinate and trusted operative. He has developed a working relationship with the party, providing intelligence and tactical support. His geas ties him directly to [[Neverwinter]]'s interests, making him simultaneously constrained by and invested in the city's wellbeing.
