## Overview

The Watcher is a tall, tattered figure bound to the [[Raven Queen]]'s domain as an eternal guardian cursed to a passive role. Once possessed of functional wings, The Watcher now bears only one broken wing, with a shadowy phantom of the other wing visible in spectral form. The figure's appearance reflects centuries of servitude and constraint, dressed in tattered garments befitting their liminal existence between life, death, and divine service.

## Role in the Campaign

The Watcher serves as a gatekeeper within the [[Raven Queen]]'s fortress, controlling the mechanisms of the domain itself. Most significantly, they command the platform mechanisms and possess the power to summon the bridge that provides access to the Raven Queen's throne room—making them essential to any who would seek audience with the goddess. However, The Watcher operates under a binding curse that prevents direct intervention in mortal affairs.

## Key Events

During the assault on the [[Raven Queen]]'s fortress, The Watcher was forced to bear witness to an undead army breaching the fortress defenses while remaining magically bound from intervening. This cruel restriction highlights the nature of their servitude—they possess knowledge and power over the domain's mechanisms but are forbidden from using it to affect the conflict directly. The frustration of this constraint underscores the tragedy of their position as an observer to catastrophic events they could theoretically prevent.
