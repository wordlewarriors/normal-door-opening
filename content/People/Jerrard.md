#Neverwinter 

Owner of [[My Chemical Reagents]]

## Overview

Jerrard is the proprietor of [[My Chemical Reagents]], an alchemical supply shop located in [[Neverwinter]]. He presents himself as a straightforward merchant focused on his business operations and maintaining good relations with local authorities through tax arrangements.

## Role in the Campaign

Jerrard became a key figure in the party's investigation during the [[Plague Investigation]] arc when they discovered connections between his shop and suspicious activity involving the [[Shadowfell Market]]. As a shop owner with access to materials and storage space, he became either a willing participant or an unwitting pawn in a larger scheme involving noble house politics and potentially dangerous goods trafficking.

## Key Events

When questioned about connections to Drow records from the [[Shadowfell Market]], Jerrard claimed genuine surprise at such involvement. He revealed he had been storing four to five wooden crates bearing [[Lord Neverember]]'s official seal in his shop over approximately one and a half weeks. In exchange for this storage service, he had negotiated a tax rebate from the city authorities—a mutually beneficial arrangement that suggested legitimate business.

Jerrard disclosed that an elven man had been making regular deliveries of these crates. More concerningly, he suspected that someone had been accessing the crates through a back goods door, suggesting the contents were either being removed or the crates themselves were serving as a cover for illicit activity.

## Relationships

Jerrard maintains a business relationship with [[Lord Neverember]] or his representatives, evidenced by the sealed crates and the tax rebate arrangement. He has an ongoing interaction with an unnamed elven man who served as the delivery contact. His relationship with the party is one of cautious cooperation, as he appears willing to share information about the suspicious activity occurring in his shop.
