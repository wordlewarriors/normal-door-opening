## Overview

Aust's mother was a respected member of her forest community, known for her skill and dedication as a scout. She undertook extensive scouting expeditions that kept her away from the settlement for considerable periods, demonstrating both competence and trusted standing among her people. Her death marked a turning point in [[Aust]]'s personal journey and the fate of the forest community.

## Supernatural Awareness

Prior to her death, Aust's mother experienced recurring visions of wolves circling a great oak, suggesting some form of mystical sensitivity or connection to the natural world. She also noticed unusual animal behavior—creatures migrating out of season—which she recognized as an omen of something amiss. These signs preceded the catastrophic orc attack on her community, though their true meaning was not fully understood until after the assault.

## Death and Aftermath

During an orc raid on the forest settlement, Aust's mother was struck instantly by a bolt of lightning in what appeared to be a coordinated attack. Her death was not the end of her presence, however. She was dragged away by a talos orc while [[Aust]] watched in horror and helplessness, unable to prevent the desecration of her body or properly mourn her passing.

## Spiritual Resolution

Aust's mother appeared as a luminous white apparition during [[Aust]]'s soul trial, manifesting as a spiritual entity caught between two paths. Faced with the choice between vengeance and peace, she ultimately chose peace. Her spirit was laid to rest in the river, where it dispersed into healing waters, finally finding tranquility after her violent death.
