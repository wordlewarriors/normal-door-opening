## Overview

Ivaran Sylhorn is the publicly recognized Minister for Health on the [[Council of Neverwinter]], presenting himself as a proud pure-blood high elf. However, investigation has revealed his true identity: [[Bergst Tsarran]] of House T'sarran, a Drow noble orchestrating a catastrophic conspiracy from beneath layers of deception and magical concealment. His manor in the [[Protector's Enclave]] serves as the nexus for his operations, disguised as the residence of a respected council member while harboring dark magical artifacts, necromantic research, and evidence of worship devoted to [[Kyransalee]].

## Role in the Campaign

Sylhorn operates as a high-level antagonist manipulating [[Neverwinter]]'s institutions from within the Council itself. His position as Health Minister granted him access to resources, records, and legitimacy to pursue his true agenda—researching fungal networks, shadow magic, and the logistics of an ascension ritual involving a world-destroying Orb. Through forged documents, he has maintained recruitment connections to both the [[Grey Cloaks]] and the [[Raven Queen Inquisition]], suggesting broader conspiracies beyond Neverwinter. His careful pattern of visits to alchemical suppliers, the [[Shrouded Ledger]], [[Whispering Vials]], and the [[Raven's Nest]] warehouse revealed a methodical criminal infrastructure supporting his dark magical research and artifact acquisition.

## Key Events

[[Aarlon]] first suspected Sylhorn of corruption, observing his suspicious movement patterns between his mansion and various shops and storage facilities. Investigation of trade logs revealed contradictions to his claimed pure-blood heritage. The party discovered his long-term rental of unit 17C at [[Raven's Nest]], concealing alchemy reagents, scrolls, and a necromantic key with an emerald alongside blueprints for an abyssal-binding artifact. An underground necromantic hatchery contained maps marking his manor as a convergence point of strategic importance. Physical inspection revealed his estate protected by powerful magical wards, monitored by multiple factions, and infested with rune-marked magical spiders. His manor's study contained a portrait that functions as an active scrying and surveillance tool, while his collection exposed obsession with shadow magic, fungal research, and elaborate forgeries. Ultimately revealed as Bergst Tsarran, he has been scrying on the party for 24-36 hours and is orchestrating an ascension ritual designed to destroy the world and enable his rebirth.
