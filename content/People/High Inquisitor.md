## Overview

The High Inquisitor is a senior cleric of the [[Raven Queen]] stationed within the religious hierarchy of [[Neverwinter]]. He has held positions at both the [[Neverwinter Temple of the Raven Queen]] and [[Neverdeath Graveyard]], suggesting significant ecclesiastical rank and authority over death-related religious matters in the city. His current whereabouts and status remain uncertain as of the latest reports.

## Role in the Campaign

The High Inquisitor has been deeply involved in investigating and combating the plague that devastated [[Neverwinter]]. During the height of the crisis, he conducted forbidden magical research into the nature of plague-infected undead, using divination magic to read the thoughts of these creatures. He maintains or maintained a secret underground chamber beneath the [[Neverwinter Temple of the Raven Queen]] containing alchemical reagents and divination materials necessary for this work.

More recently, he was posted to [[Neverdeath Graveyard]], where he may have overseen funerary rites. His involvement in death magic rituals at the graveyard has raised concerns that he was either corrupted or deceived into enabling the corrupted death magic now affecting that location.

## Key Events

The High Inquisitor experienced severe trauma from a [[Sangrullo]] attack, which left him deeply frightened and psychologically marked. During the plague crisis, he reported that his divine connection to the [[Raven Queen]] was weakening intermittently—a troubling sign that may indicate broader spiritual corruption or a crisis of faith. His divination work revealed that [[Neverwinter]] was losing approximately 100 people per night to the plague at its peak.

When scouts were sent to investigate [[Neverdeath Graveyard]], they were swarmed by star spawn before they could reach the mausoleums where the High Inquisitor may have been sheltering. His current fate remains unknown.

## Relationships

[[Sister Maelin Thorn]] knew of the High Inquisitor vaguely through ecclesiastical circles, recognizing him as occupying a much higher position in the religious hierarchy of [[Neverwinter]] than her own station.
