## Overview

Guildmistress Rayleen is the leader of [[Neverwinter]]'s Alchemists' Guild, holding a position of significant authority and influence over the magical reagent supply in the city. She is deeply concerned with the practical challenges facing her guild and the wider community dependent on alchemical services.

Rayleen serves as a critical resource contact regarding the alchemical infrastructure of [[Neverwinter]]. As guildmistress, she possesses detailed knowledge of supply chains, production capacity, and the current state of magical reagent availability. She has positioned herself as an advocate for addressing systemic shortages that threaten healing potion production, making her instrumental in identifying and solving the city's reagent crisis.
