## 📖 Major Story Arcs

### Return to Neverwinter *(current)*

The party crashed back into a Neverwinter transformed by catastrophe. The city's protective barrier has shrunk, the dead are no longer passing to the afterlife, and Nihal — wounded but not destroyed — hangs in the sky above the city. Nand is presumed dead. The Neverwinter Academy is paralysed by internal faction disputes, the House of Knowledge is split between preservationists and those who want to weaponise forbidden texts, and the city guard are dying in droves against unkillable Starspawn. Lord Neverember has invested the party with full civic authority and will ride with them to Neverdeath Graveyard, where corrupted funerary rites may hold the key to freeing the dead. Merric's dreams warn of one unresolved story within his clan, while Ultrin's book writes in his own hand without his knowledge — evidence that his absorption of the Weave carries consequences yet to be understood.

### Nand's Gambit & The Soul Trial Finale

The figure known as the Ferryman was revealed to be Archmage Nandntiersanon, who had spent decades preparing a planar alignment spell capable of wounding Nihal. He guided the party through visions of the planes — Zugtamoid strangling the Araumycos, Grageon and Mr Johnson slowly dying in defence of the Sharn — before revealing himself and handing the spell to Ultrin. At The Farm Realm, with Nand sacrificing himself to fuel the casting, the party fought off worm-spawn while Ultrin redirected the Weave-weapon at Nihal. The star retreated wounded. But Ultrin absorbed a fragment of the Weave in the process, and was simultaneously tempted by a darker voice toward godhood — a temptation whose echo has not yet faded.

### River of Souls & Redemption Trials

After the Far Realm conflict, the party journeyed along the River of Souls. Each member faced a personal trial: Aust chose to set his mother's soul free; Merric confronted his vengeance and carved his own path toward redemption; and Jorund was confronted with the consequences of his past deceptions, seeing a cathedral built from his misdeeds. These trials allowed the characters to confront and reconcile their histories, altering the tapestry of fate — and, as the Ferryman noted, changing the river itself.

### Far Realm Incursion & Starspawn

Destroying Ivaran's shadow urns weakened barriers between worlds. During the starspawn battle, Archmage Nand exploited the breach, using a sphere of annihilation to rip open a portal to the Far Realm. Time and reality warped, with cosmic strands linking the heroes' souls. This event nearly unleashed otherworldly horrors, forcing the party to confront nightmares and forging their resolve for the Soul Trials.

### Ivaran Sylhorn's Conspiracy

An ambitious elven alchemist and noble, Ivaran Sylhorn secretly orchestrated a plague using worm‑infested healing potions and sought godhood through an Ascension ritual. The party uncovered his necromantic experiments at apothecaries, deciphered his coded recipes, and discovered a blueprint for a sphere of annihilation. Investigations at his manor revealed vials designed to "taint the Weave" and notes linking Araumycos fungi to shadow magic. In 2025 they learned he was Bergst of Drow House Tsarran and planned to use shadow vessels as conduits. His ritual culminated atop his manor, where the heroes destroyed the shadow urns and prevented his Ascension, though it opened a rift to the Far Realm that Nand later exploited.

### The Plague & Council Corruption

A mysterious plague ravaged Neverwinter in 2024. Healing potions were ineffective and My Chemical Reagents' elixirs contained tiny eggs. The Archdruid warned that Araumycos had been corrupted by Kyuss and Zuggtmoy worshippers. Investigations revealed tainted potions across the city and shady dealings by the Council's health and trade ministers. The Circle of 9s and other clandestine groups manipulated events behind the scenes. This arc ties directly into Ivaran's conspiracy, culminating in the destruction of his hatchery and the purge of infected supplies.

### Early Shadowfell Saga

In the early campaign (2022), the party journeyed into the Shadowfell—the dark mirror of the world—seeking to thwart the Forge‑Master and ally with undead factions. They disguised allies as undead, confronted ghouls, and lost party member Izz. This saga established the group's unity and set the tone for their later struggles against necromancy and corruption.
