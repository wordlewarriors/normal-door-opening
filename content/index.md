# Campaign Summary: Wordle Warriors – Up to 22 July 2025

## 🧭 Overview

The story follows a group of adventurers (Aust, Merric, Jorund, and Ultrin) from the depths of the Underdark through a plague-ridden Neverwinter and finally into the River Styx, where their souls undergo judgment. Themes include corruption, death, memory, and redemption.

---

## 📜 Act 1: Underdark & Glumgluts (2022)

### Early Challenges
- Begins in **Glumgluts Manor** under Drow control.
- Merric receives a **black crack** across his arm.
- Wizard **Izz** disappears after a cosmic vision.

### The Forge-Master Arc
- Navigate **rune-wards**, battle the **Forge-Master**.
- With help from a **forest troll**, the group survives.
- Duanaogar the Defier, a ghoul king, offers a feast.

### Prophecy and Warning
- Marka the Dead is just a pawn of **Kiaransalee**.
- Larger war foreshadowed across Underdark and surface.

## 🧪 Act 2: Plague in Neverwinter (2024)

### Discovering the Plague
- Plague kills citizens, creates undead.
- Infected **healing potions** traced to Council crates.

### Druidic and Arcane Warnings
- **Archdruid** connects plague to **Araumycos**, **Kyuss**, **Far Realm**.

### Political Intrigue
- Council divided: suspicion on **Ivaran Sylhorn** and **Amelia Briar**.
- Links to **Lord Neverember** and dead drops.

### Black Spider Network
- Uncover **worm hatchery** in speakeasy.
- Map points to Ivaran’s manor and tainted potion supply chain.

#neverwinter #plague #ivaransylhorn #council #kyuss #araumycos #2024 #investigation

## 🕯 Act 3: Ivaran's Manor & the Ascension Ritual (2024–2025)

### Secrets of the Manor
- Rooms reveal worship of **Lolth**, **Kiaransalee**, and **necromancy**.
- Discover **tainted potion crates**, false IDs, magical documents.

### The Ascension
- Rooftop ritual with **sphere of annihilation** and “vessels”.
- **Merric** seizes the sphere to disrupt the ritual.

### Battle on the Rooftop
- Enemy becomes **starspawn**. 
- **Archmage Nandntiersanon** arrives, wounds the **Far Realm** deity.
- Manor collapses; souls separated into River Styx.

#ivaransylhorn #ascension #starspawn #nandntiersanon #farrealm #2025 #campaign-climax

## ⚰ Act 4: The River Styx & Soul Trials (2025)

### Arrival on the River
- Party wakes on a **River Styx skiff** with the **Ferryman**.
- Warned that their souls are tainted by past actions and powers.

### Aust's Trial
- Childhood memory of orc raid and mother’s death.
- Chooses **peace and protection** over vengeance.

### Merric's Trial
- Reenacts burial rite gone wrong.
- Confronts vengeance vs peace and **carves his own path**.

### Jorund's Trial (In Progress)
- Inside a **bone cathedral**.
- Faced with stained-glass scenes of fraud, lies, and divine betrayal.
- Confronted by the **ghost of a child** he deceived.
