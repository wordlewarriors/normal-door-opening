# 🗺️ Wordle Warriors: Campaign Index
> This vault tracks the story of the Wordle Warriors D&D campaign, spanning from the depths of the Underdark to the soul trials of the River Styx. This summary is organised in **reverse chronological order**, beginning with the most recent events.
---
## 📅 2025 – River Styx & Soul Trials
### July 2025 — Soul Judgments
- The party awakens, dead, on a **skiff drifting down the River Styx**.
- The **Ferryman** explains their souls are tainted and must be judged.
- [[2025-07-15]]: **Aust** relives the orc raid that killed his mother, choosing peace.
- [[2025-07-18]]: **Merric** undergoes a soul trial and claims agency.
- [[2025-07-22]]: **Jorund** begins his judgment in a bone cathedral.
- Ultrin’s trial is pending; his pact remains a mystery.

### April–June 2025 — The Ascension & Death
- [[2025-04-01]]: The party interrupts a ritual involving urns and a sphere of annihilation.
- [[2025-04-08]]: The ritual backfires, summoning a **starspawn** and killing the party.
- [[2025-04-15]]: **Archmage Nand** wounds the Far Realm. The group dies, souls cast into the Styx.
