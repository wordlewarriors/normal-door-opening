## Overview

The Forest Village is a settlement inhabited primarily by elves, nestled within a natural woodland setting. It served as the childhood home of [[Aust]] and was known for its distinctive architecture and integration with the natural environment. The village was devastated during the Neverwinter arc when it fell under siege by an invading orc force.

## Description

The village features distinctive architectural elements adapted to its forest environment, including burrow homes built into the earth and a prominent large tree that served as a focal point of the settlement. The construction style reflects the elven inhabitants' harmony with nature, utilizing natural features and materials from the surrounding woodland to create their dwellings.

## Key Events

The Forest Village was attacked and set on fire by invading orcs during Session 28. The assault resulted in a catastrophic loss of life, with most of the village's inhabitants killed in the conflict. Among the casualties were [[Elorian]] and [[Mira]], both significant figures in the village's community and in [[Aust]]'s personal history. This devastating attack fundamentally changed the trajectory of the campaign and left lasting consequences for the surviving inhabitants.
