## Overview

The Druid Grove is a sacred forest sanctuary located outside [[Neverwinter]]. It serves as a refuge for practitioners of natural magic and a haven for woodland creatures. The grove centers around an ancient, massive tree of considerable magical significance. Recently, the grove has become a site of devastation following an attack by extraplanar entities.

## Description

The grove is characterized by dense forest growth with an enormous central tree that towers above the surrounding woodland. This great tree appears to possess consciousness and magical properties that allow it to interact with its environment in unusual ways. The forest canopy is thick, and the grove typically teems with wildlife and natural vitality. However, following the recent attack, the grove has become eerily quiet—most animal life has fled or vanished, leaving behind an unsettling silence where birdsong and rustling creatures once thrived.

## Key Events

The grove was recently attacked by [[Starspawn of Nihal]], alien entities of unknown origin. During this assault, the grove's great tree was awakened, seemingly gaining or demonstrating conscious agency. One of the Starspawn creatures currently remains trapped or held within the branches of this ancient tree, suggesting the tree may have defended itself or been used as a containment vessel. The druids who normally inhabit and maintain the grove have disappeared under unclear circumstances, leaving the sanctuary abandoned and vulnerable.
