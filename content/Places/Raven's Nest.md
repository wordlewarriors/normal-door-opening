## Overview

Raven's Nest is a large storage facility located in the Dock district of [[Neverwinter]], operating as a warehouse with hundreds of individual storage units distributed across multiple levels. The facility ranges from smaller 10x10 units to expansive 30x30 spaces, catering to various clientele with storage needs. Rental rates are standardized at 60 gold pieces per day with no long-term discount options available, making it an expensive option for extended storage. Access between levels is provided by external wooden stairs that run along the warehouse's exterior.

## Description

The warehouse presents a utilitarian structure typical of dock-district commerce, with weathered wooden stairs providing the primary means of vertical access. The facility's layout accommodates numerous individual units across multiple stories, suggesting substantial square footage and capable storage capacity. The building operates with notably loose security measures, allowing relatively easy access to the premises compared to more regulated establishments.
