## Overview

The Necromantic Hatchery is a clandestine 10x10 foot laboratory concealed beneath a speakeasy, accessible only through a warded metal door. The facility serves as a breeding ground for unknown necromantic entities and a repository for corrupted alchemical resources. Its discovery suggests an organized effort to cultivate dark magical forces within [[Neverwinter]].

## Description

The hatchery's cramped interior is lined with shelves containing numerous jars filled with greenish-red liquid, within which eggs, worms, and larvae develop in various stages of growth. A central tank dominates the space, actively housing writhing worms that appear to be the primary focus of cultivation efforts. An enchanter's workbench occupies one corner, complete with ritual circles drawn or etched into the floor and surrounding surfaces, indicating preparation for significant magical working.

A substantial cache of several hundred healing potions is stored within the facility. Notably, many of these potions bear signs of necromantic corruption, their effectiveness compromised or perverted by dark magical influence. The presence of both the growing specimens and the tainted potions suggests the hatchery may serve a dual purpose: both breeding ground and distribution point for corrupted supplies.
