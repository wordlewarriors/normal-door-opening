## Overview

The Hill with the Great Tree is a sacred gathering place within the lands of [[Merric]]'s clan, serving as the spiritual and practical heart of their community. Located in the clan's ancestral territory, this site holds deep cultural significance as the setting for important ceremonies and traditions that bind the clan together. The presence of the great tree has made this location a natural focal point for clan life across generations.

## Description

The great tree stands as the centerpiece of this location, its trunk and branches carved with the names of tribal ancestors and significant clan members. These tribal inscriptions serve as a living record of the clan's heritage and lineage. The tree is surrounded by homes built in concentric circles, creating a protective ring around this natural monument. During gatherings, the area is illuminated by lanterns, creating an atmospheric space for ceremonial occasions and communal assemblies.

## Practical Significance

Beyond its ceremonial importance, the great tree provides a vital resource for the clan's material needs. The tree supplies wood that the clan harvests sustainably for construction, tools, and fuel. This dual purpose—as both a sacred symbol and a practical resource provider—reinforces the clan's deep connection to the land and their philosophy of working in harmony with nature rather than against it.
