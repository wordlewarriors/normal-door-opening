## Overview

SoulEarth is a mystical realm accessible through ritual magic, existing as a parallel plane where souls undergo trials and transformation. Entry into this realm carries significant risks—those who cross while bearing a [[worm seed]] or other taint will find their souls corrupted by the passage itself, even if the ritual succeeds. The realm appears to respond to the spiritual state and actions of those who enter it.

## Description

SoulEarth possesses a landscape that is far from static. Its most prominent feature is a river that normally appears void-like and featureless, reflecting nothing. However, the realm's geography can shift dramatically based on events within it—the landscape is capable of reshaping itself into otherworldly structures of considerable beauty and strangeness, such as towering columns of colored glass that emerge seemingly from nowhere. The realm has a deeply introspective quality, as if the very environment responds to the inner state of those who traverse it.

## Key Events

[[Merric]] experienced a significant vision in which he felt himself entering SoulEarth during a sequence in which his fate was being tested or altered. His actions within the realm—specifically his alteration of his own fate—had profound consequences for the realm itself. Following Merric's fateful choice, the void-like river transformed into a reflective surface, suggesting that changes made within SoulEarth can have lasting effects on its fundamental nature. This transformation implies that the realm is not merely a static trial ground but a place where meaningful choices generate tangible, permanent alterations to reality.
