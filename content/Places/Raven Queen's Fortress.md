## Overview

The Raven Queen's Fortress is a divine stronghold located within the [[Raven Queen]]'s domain, serving as a repository of memories and a sanctuary for lost souls. The fortress exists as a liminal space between the mortal realm and the divine, filled with countless ravens that serve as extensions of the Raven Queen's will and witnesses to the deeds of the dead. The structure itself appears to be woven from shadow, starlight, and the essence of forgotten moments.

## Description

The fortress embodies contradictions—simultaneously ancient and eternal, solid yet ethereal. Its architecture defies conventional geometry, with towers that curve impossibly and halls that seem to shift between visits. Ravens of various sizes perch upon every surface, their eyes glowing with an eerie intelligence. The atmosphere within is heavy with the weight of countless memories, creating a melancholic yet strangely reverent ambiance. Lost souls wander the corridors, appearing as translucent figures caught in moments of their final days or greatest triumphs.

## Key Events

During the [[Plague Investigation]] arc, the fortress trembled violently as [[Nihal]] encroached upon its sacred boundaries, disrupting the delicate spiritual balance maintained within its walls. This intrusion coincided with [[Jorund]]'s soul trial—a critical test that would determine his spiritual fate.
