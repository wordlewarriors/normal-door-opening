## Overview

The Dark Stone Chapel is an ancient underground chamber hidden beneath [[Neverwinter]], constructed in a time when halflings held significant presence in the region. The chapel features distinctive architecture marked by carved halfling runes and serves as a sacred—though now corrupted—burial site. The chamber has been repurposed for dark rituals, most notably those involving the [[Master of Locks]].

## Description

The chapel is a damp stone chamber carved from living rock, its walls bearing intricate halfling runes whose original purpose and meanings remain obscure. The most striking feature is a circle of mushrooms carved or arranged across the ceiling, creating an unsettling canopy above the chamber floor. Braziers positioned throughout the space hold smoldering incense, filling the chapel with thick, heavy smoke that adds to the oppressive atmosphere. The moisture-laden air and ancient stonework suggest the chamber has existed undisturbed for centuries, preserved in darkness far below the city streets.

## Key Events

The chapel became the site of a corrupted halfling burial rite connected to the activities of the [[Master of Locks]]. This perversion of what were once sacred funerary practices represents a significant desecration of halfling religious traditions. The specifics of the ritual and its purpose remain tied to larger events unfolding during the party's investigation of [[Neverwinter]]'s darker secrets.
