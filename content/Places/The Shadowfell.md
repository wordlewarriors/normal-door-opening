## Overview

The [[Shadowfell]] is an otherworldly realm that exists between the material plane and the [[Far Realms]]. It serves as a liminal space where souls are processed and judged before moving forward to their final destinations. The realm can be glimpsed through magical means, particularly through artifacts of significant power like the [[sphere of annihilation]].

## Description

The [[Shadowfell]] appears as a realm of grand architecture and divine judgment. At its heart stands a massive fortress—a place of solemn purpose where the fates of souls are determined. The realm itself exists in a state between worlds, neither fully material nor completely ethereal, making it inaccessible to the living under normal circumstances. Its appearance is rendered in muted tones and possesses an otherworldly quality distinct from the material plane.

## Key Events

During Session 26, the cleric [[Ultrin]] experienced a vision that revealed the structure and purpose of the [[Shadowfell]]. Through this divinely-granted sight, [[Ultrin]] witnessed the fortress of judgment and gained understanding of the realm's role in the cosmic order. This vision provided the party with knowledge that the [[Shadowfell]] could potentially be accessed or observed through the [[sphere of annihilation]], suggesting the artifact acts as a window between worlds.
