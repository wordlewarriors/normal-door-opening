## Overview

Anyfing + Everyfing is a peculiar bargain store located in the [[Blacklake District]] of [[Neverwinter]]. Despite its whimsical name, the establishment operates as a front for a more shadowy enterprise, combining legitimate retail operations with black market dealings conducted through its cellar level.

## Description

The store presents itself as a discount merchant offering an eclectic mix of goods and curiosities. Its inventory is deliberately non-standard and unpredictable, featuring health potions housed in novelty containers rather than conventional vials—a marketing choice that obscures the true nature of some products. Poison vials are also stocked, adding to the morally ambiguous character of the operation.

The cellar serves as the nerve center of the establishment's black market activities, where the proprietors handle transactions and maintain connections with underground suppliers. The contrast between the casual, browsing-friendly upper floors and the secretive operations below allows the business to maintain plausible deniability regarding its shadier dealings.

## Operations

Anyfing + Everyfing obtains much of its inventory through unconventional channels. Primary sources include black market connections with smugglers and criminal networks, as well as acquisitions from house estate sales—legitimate purchases of property collections that allow them to resell goods at bargain prices while occasionally mixing in more questionable merchandise.

This dual sourcing strategy allows the store to maintain an appearance of legitimacy while quietly serving clientele seeking harder-to-obtain alchemical substances and poisons without drawing attention from authorities.
