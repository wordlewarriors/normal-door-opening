## Overview

A realm of madness that is far from the day to day events of the campaign and the prime material plane. Likely home of [[Nihal]] and the [[Starspawn of Nihal]].

The Far Realm is an otherworldly dimension at the outer limits of reality, a place of impossible geometry and eldritch nature that lies beyond the void of mortal skies. It is fundamentally alien to the material plane and is the dwelling place of dead gods and elder evils. It can only be reached through powerful planar magic such as [[Nand]]'s planar alignment spell.

## Description

The Far Realm defies conventional description. It appears as a landscape of cosmic death and corruption, with dead suns and vast, alien stars scattered across an incomprehensible expanse, and a distinctly red and crackly look. The environment is hostile and unstable, and can react violently when disturbed by outsiders. What inhabits it is largely unknowable, and it is unclear whether it is a single entity, many beings or something beyond that distinction.

One of its most notable inhabitants is [[Nihal]], a reborn star entity covered entirely in worms, which suggests the realm is infested with parasitic elder evils.

## Key Events

During the [[Plague Investigation]] arc, [[Nand]]'s golden-black bolt pierced the boundaries of normal existence and struck something within the Far Realm, wounding a god. That should be impossible by all known natural and magical laws, and it breached barriers previously thought absolute. Whether it was deliberate or accidental, and whether it was effective or merely wounding, remains under investigation.

The party was later taken to the Far Realm by [[Nand]]'s planar alignment spell during the campaign against the worm infestation. On the deck of their ship they assembled a spell circle at the prow and performed a final ritual against the worms and [[Nihal]]. The ritual provoked a violent eruption from the worm star and catastrophic disturbances in the immediate area.

Afterwards the creatures of the Far Realm showed awareness of the party. When [[Ultrin]] opened his shutters, he saw hideous entities from beyond the barrier turn to look directly at him.
