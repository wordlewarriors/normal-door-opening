## Overview

The High Forest is [[Aust]]'s homeland, a place of deep spiritual and personal significance. It serves as both a physical location and a realm where the boundaries between the material and spiritual grow thin. The forest is tied to [[Aust]]'s identity and connection to his ancestry, making it a natural conduit for trials of the soul and visions of profound meaning.

## Description

The High Forest is characterized by ancient, towering oak trees, including a great oak tree of particular significance. During [[Aust]]'s vision, the forest was bathed in golden light, creating an ethereal and sacred atmosphere. The landscape embodies natural beauty and spiritual power, though it is not immune to corruption. The forest appears to exist partially in the material plane and partially in a more mystical realm where visions and trials manifest.
