## Overview

My Chemical Reagents is an alchemical supply shop located in [[Neverwinter]]'s Protector's Enclave, owned by [[Jerrard]]. The establishment operates as both a legitimate business selling alchemical components and a front for more sinister activities. The shop has become a focal point of investigation for the party due to its connections to [[Lord Neverember]] and suspicious contraband.

## Description

The shop features a distinctive layout with a dedicated storage area, making it a recognizable landmark in the Protector's Enclave. [[Merric]] and [[Jorund]] have used it as a reference point when surveying similar establishments in the district, suggesting it has notable architectural or operational characteristics that distinguish it from other apothecaries.

## Key Events

The shop served as a storage facility for mysterious crates bearing [[Lord Neverember]]'s seals, discovered to contain watered-down healing potions mixed with suspicious egg-like objects—items connected to the plague investigation. In a dramatic turn, the establishment was deliberately burned down by a [[Drow]] operative acting under orders from someone bearing [[Lord Neverember]]'s seal, destroying evidence and eliminating the location as an investigation point. The party's assigned watcher failed to prevent or even notice the arson, suggesting either skilled execution or inadequate surveillance.

The party has conducted stakeout operations at the location and recruited assistance from [[Aarlon]] to maintain observation of the premises.

## Notable Connections

My Chemical Reagents operates in the import/export trade, though it notably appears in none of the [[Leonard family]]'s extensive trade logs or rumour files—an absence that itself suggests intentional obscurity or deliberate separation from known trading networks.
