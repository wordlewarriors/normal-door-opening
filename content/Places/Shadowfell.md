## Overview

The Shadowfell is a dark reflection of the material plane, existing as a mirror realm of shadow and despair. It serves as a nexus point for undead activity and darker magics. In the context of the party's investigations, it has become significant as a location with connections to both the plague outbreak and suspicious mercantile activities centered around [[Jerrard]] and mysterious crates stored in [[Neverwinter]].

## Description

This plane manifests as a twisted, shadowed version of the world, suffused with necrotic energy and perpetual gloom. Within its bounds exists a functioning market—an unexpected bastion of commerce amid the darkness—where records and goods flow between those who traffic in forbidden knowledge and darker dealings. The market operates as a hub for information brokers and merchants willing to deal in subjects most would shun.

## Key Events

[[Jorund]] has claimed responsibility for eliminating a significant population of undead creatures within the Shadowfell, suggesting either a major military incursion or personal involvement in combat operations there. This claim raises questions about the extent of undead activity in the realm and what prompted such action.

The market located within the Shadowfell contains crucial records that connect to [[Jerrard]]'s activities and the storage of mysterious crates in [[Neverwinter]]. These records may provide vital leads in understanding the nature of the crates, their contents, and Jerrard's role in the plague investigation. The existence of such documentation in this shadow market suggests organized activity and deliberate record-keeping by individuals operating in the shadows.
