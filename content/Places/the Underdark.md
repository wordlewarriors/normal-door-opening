## Overview

The Underdark is a vast subterranean realm beneath the surface world, stretching far beneath known lands. This underground domain is home to numerous civilizations, creatures, and phenomena both ancient and dangerous. In the region below [[Neverwinter]] and surrounding areas, the Underdark has become a site of critical concern due to the presence of [[Zugtamoid]], a powerful entity that has seized control of the [[Araumycos]] fungal network.

## Key Events

[[Zugtamoid]] has successfully overtaken the [[Araumycos]] in this region of the Underdark and is actively working to spread its influence further. This represents a significant threat, as the Araumycos is a vast interconnected fungal system that could potentially serve as a conduit for Zugtamoid's expansion across a much larger area.

[[Aust]] discovered evidence of Zugtamoid's control during his time with the [[Sharn]], learning that the entity had already taken command of the Araumycos in this particular region. This intelligence suggests the threat has been developing for some time and may already be deeply rooted in the Underdark's fungal infrastructure.

## Notable Locations

The [[Araumycos]] fungal network is a dominant feature of this section of the Underdark, serving as both a biological system and a potential vector for Zugtamoid's influence.
