## Overview

The Far Realms exist at the absolute outer limits of reality, a place of impossible geometry and eldritch nature. During the [[Plague Investigation]] arc, [[Nand]]'s golden-black bolt—a weapon of seemingly divine origin—managed to pierce through the boundaries of normal existence and strike something within this realm. This unprecedented attack wounded a god, an event that should be impossible by all known natural and magical laws. The very fact that such a strike could land suggests the Far Realms operate under fundamentally different rules than the material world.

## Description

The Far Realms defy conventional description, existing beyond the comprehension of mortal minds. The realm itself appears to function on alien principles, indifferent to the concerns and laws of the material world. Whatever exists there operates on a scale and with a nature that mortal understanding struggles to grasp.
