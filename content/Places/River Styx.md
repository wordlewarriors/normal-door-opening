## Overview

The River Styx is a legendary waterway that serves as a mystical passage between worlds, particularly functioning as the crossing point for souls after death. According to [[The Ferryman]], gods hold no power upon the river itself, making it a uniquely neutral ground even in matters of divine consequence. The river transitions between different states—beginning as a void where ethereal forms are visible beneath the surface, then gradually becoming visible water before flowing toward [[Pandemonium]].

## Description

The River Styx manifests as a void filled with distorted bodies and faces visible beneath its surface, creating an unsettling passage between the material world and the afterlife. As one travels along it, the void gradually transforms into perceivable water. The river is marked by unusual geographical phenomena, including whirlpools of white light that simultaneously drain into and spill out of the planar boundaries, suggesting connections to multiple planes of existence. The flow of the river eventually directs toward [[Pandemonium]], indicating its role as a crucial junction in the cosmology of the campaign world.
