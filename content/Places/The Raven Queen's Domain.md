## Overview

The Raven Queen's Domain is a divine realm existing outside normal reality, accessible only through supernatural means such as stained glass visions. It serves as the seat of power for the [[Raven Queen]], a deity of death and fate. The domain operates under its own laws of existence, where conventional geometry and physics do not apply.

## Description

The domain centers on a towering fortress surrounded by impossible geometric structures that defy mortal comprehension. Most striking is the sun that illuminates this realm—yet casts shadows in multiple directions simultaneously, defying logical light source behavior. The architecture combines elements of both magnificent divine design and ominous foreboding, reflecting the duality of death and fate that the [[Raven Queen]] governs.

The landscape itself seems unstable and abstract, with spatial relationships that would be impossible in the material plane. Colors and dimensions shift in ways that challenge mortal perception, creating an otherworldly atmosphere that impresses upon visitors the alien nature of divine realms.

## Key Events

The domain recently came under siege by an enormous undead army of catastrophic scale. This force is commanded by skeletal dragons of immense power, accompanied by giant worms that tunnel through the impossible terrain. The fortress itself shows signs of severe damage and deterioration, with its defenders struggling against overwhelming numbers. The siege represents an existential threat to the domain, with the fortress appearing to be on the verge of complete collapse, suggesting that forces of significant power are arrayed against the [[Raven Queen]] and her divine authority.
