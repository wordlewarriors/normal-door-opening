## Overview

Alexander's Mystic Manuscripts is a legitimate magical bookshop in [[Neverwinter]] dedicated to advancing the study of arcane magic while maintaining ethical standards. The shop operates across multiple floors and serves as a trusted resource for adventurers, wizards, and spellcasters seeking magical knowledge. Unlike many magical retailers, the establishment prioritizes the welfare of the city and maintains strict principles regarding what materials they will and will not sell.

## Services and Inventory

The shop offers a comprehensive range of magical texts and services, including spell scrolls, wizard spellbooks, and custom scribing services. Customers can commission scribes to reproduce spells or create new spellbooks tailored to their needs. The inventory includes both common and rare magical texts, though the proprietors carefully curate their stock to prevent dangerous magic from falling into unscrupulous hands.

## Ethical Standards

Alexander's Mystic Manuscripts refuses to sell evil spells as a general policy, though the staff acknowledges that justified circumstances may warrant exceptions. This ethical stance distinguishes the shop from less scrupulous magical retailers and reflects the proprietors' commitment to [[Neverwinter]]'s safety and prosperity. The shop's staff actively educate customers about magical dangers and maintain vigorous oversight of their inventory.

## Warnings About Dangerous Texts

The staff issue serious warnings about evil books bound in leather that are allegedly crafted by [[Vecna]]. According to the shop's knowledge, these cursed tomes possess the ability to sense through their bindings and potentially exert control over their readers. Such books represent an extreme danger and are actively avoided by the shop, which refuses to stock them under any circumstances.
