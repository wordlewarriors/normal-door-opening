## Overview

The Bone Cathedral is a monumental structure located at the terminus of a vast landscape dotted with glass pillars. It stands as a striking architectural anomaly, constructed primarily from bone and glass rather than traditional stone or wood. The cathedral bears no affiliation with any established religion, instead serving as a site of dark ritual and spiritual significance tied to [[Orcus]].

The surrounding landscape consists of towering glass pillars that lead to the cathedral's entrance, creating an otherworldly approach to the structure. The combination of bone and glass creates an unsettling aesthetic—beautiful yet undeniably sinister—that reflects the dark purposes the cathedral serves.
