## Overview

The Neverwinter Temple of the Raven Queen stands as a major religious center dedicated to the goddess of death, fate, and winter in [[Neverwinter]]. Located within the [[Neverdeath Graveyard]], the temple occupies a position of significant spiritual and practical importance to the city, serving both the faithful and functioning as a center for investigations into supernatural phenomena affecting the region.

## Description

The temple features a prominent main structure typical of Raven Queen devotion, adorned with imagery of ravens and symbols of death and fate. The most notable architectural feature is a magically locked trapdoor concealed within the temple's upper levels, which provides access to a substantial basement chamber. This underground space is equipped with alchemical workstations, storage for various reagents and divination materials, and private quarters or study areas for the High Inquisitor's work.

## Key Events

During investigations into a mysterious plague outbreak affecting [[Neverwinter]], the temple became a focal point of inquiry. The discovery of the magically sealed basement revealed that the [[High Inquisitor]] had been conducting extensive research and mysterious activities involving infected townspeople, suggesting possible connections between the plague and the temple's operations. The underground chamber contained detailed personal notes documenting the [[High Inquisitor]]'s findings on the plague outbreak, indicating this was a center for plague investigation, though the true nature and ethics of these studies remain unclear.
