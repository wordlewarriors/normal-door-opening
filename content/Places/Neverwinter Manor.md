## Overview

[[Neverwinter Manor]], also known as [[Veilgard Manor]], serves as the party's primary residence and base of operations in [[Neverwinter]]. The property was granted to the party and is currently maintained by the dedicated butler [[Edgar]]. The manor functions as both a comfortable home and a strategic headquarters from which the party coordinates their investigations and plans.

## Description

The manor is a well-appointed mansion featuring multiple floors and specialized rooms. The third floor contains a penthouse suite with luxurious accommodations. Individual bedrooms are provided for each party member throughout the residence, each secured with a personal key. The basement houses servant quarters. The grounds include a rear area where graves are maintained, including those of [[Verna]] and another servant. The property is consistently well-maintained despite the party's frequent absences, a testament to [[Edgar]]'s dedication to his duties.

## Key Events

The manor became the party's home and served as a gathering point during their investigations into the plague affecting [[Neverwinter]]. It was here that party members received lodging and established their individual spaces. The manor has hosted numerous strategy sessions where the group recapped their findings and coordinated next steps in their investigations. The graves in the rear grounds serve as a solemn reminder of losses connected to the party's adventures.
