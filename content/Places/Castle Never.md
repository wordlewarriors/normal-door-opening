## Overview

Castle Never is the royal seat of [[Lord Neverember]]'s power in [[Neverwinter]]. It serves as both the administrative center of the city's government and the primary residence of its ruling lord. The castle houses official chambers, guest quarters, and spaces for state functions and proclamations.

## Description

The castle is an ancient structure showing significant signs of wear and damage. The western wall bears evidence of a massive impact, apparently struck by some enormous beast, with the courtyard filled with fallen stone chunks. Interior passages are lined with guards wearing polished but noticeably exhausted armor, suggesting the castle has endured recent hardship.

The architectural design reflects the castle's historical grandeur, featuring ornate spaces such as the Hall of Ashen Mirrors and a grand public room suited for formal state ceremonies. However, the castle's condition is deteriorating—lighting relies on non-magical sources, and the atmosphere is notably cold and unwelcoming. The royal guest wing, where visiting dignitaries are housed, contains ancient and worn quarters once reserved for the most esteemed guests of the royal family, though their current state reflects years of neglect.

## Key Events

All mirrors throughout the castle, including those in the Hall of Ashen Mirrors, have been deliberately destroyed, though the reasons behind this remain unclear. The castle hosted a major faction gathering where a proclamation investing the party with authority over certain matters was formally sealed and posted in the grand public room, marking an official transfer of power or responsibility.
