## Overview

Pandemonium is a chaotic plane known for its vast network of interconnected portals, earning it the designation as "the plane of a thousand portals" according to [[Ultrin]]. Most of these portals link to other regions within Pandemonium itself, creating a labyrinthine geography that defies conventional mapping. The plane serves as a convergence point for various planar routes, including the [[River Styx]], which flows into its waters as a major thoroughfare between realms.
