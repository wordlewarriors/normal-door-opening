## Overview

The Farm Realm is an otherworldly dimension that exists beyond the void of mortal skies, accessible only through powerful planar magic such as [[Nand]]'s planar alignment spell. It is fundamentally alien to the material plane, serving as the dwelling place of dead gods and elder evils. The realm is characterized by its hostile, incomprehensible nature—a place where the rules of reality seem fundamentally different from those known to mortals.

## Description

The Farm Realm appears as a landscape of cosmic death and corruption. Its most striking features include dead suns and vast, alien stars scattered across an incomprehensible expanse. The realm has a distinctly red and crackly appearance, suggesting either extreme heat, cosmic radiation, or some form of supernatural decay. The environment itself seems hostile and unstable, with the potential for violent reactions when disturbed by outsiders.

One of the realm's most notable inhabitants is [[Nihal]], a reborn star entity covered entirely in worms, suggesting that the realm is infested with parasitic elder evils or similar abominations. The very landscape may be considered a form of "farm" where these creatures breed and subsist on divine corpses.

## Key Events

The party was transported to the Farm Realm through [[Nand]]'s planar alignment spell during their campaign against the worm infestation. On the deck of their ship, they assembled a spell circle at the prow and conducted a final ritual designed to combat the worms and [[Nihal]]. The ritual provoked a violent eruption from the worm star, causing catastrophic disturbances throughout the immediate area.

After this confrontation, the creatures of the Farm Realm demonstrated awareness of the party's presence. When [[Ultrin]] opened his shutters, he witnessed hideous entities from beyond the realm's barrier turning to look directly at him, indicating that the creatures possess some form of sentience and interest in the intruders from the material plane.
