## Overview

The Whispering Vials is a renowned alchemist shop located in [[Neverwinter]], operated by a small gnomish proprietor distinguished by his elaborately twirled moustache. The establishment specializes in the creation and sale of healing potions and rare magical ingredients, though its operations have been significantly hampered by the inability to obtain necessary imports. The shop's name evokes both the subtle nature of alchemical work and the secretive dealings conducted within its walls.

## Description

The shop presents itself as a legitimate apothecary, with shelves displaying various potions and alchemical supplies for public purchase. The real work occurs in the back workshop, where distillation stations line the walls in careful arrangement. Among the active experiments sits a mysterious browny-green potion of unknown purpose, suggesting experimental work beyond standard healing remedies. The workshop is equipped for serious alchemical research and production, indicating the proprietor possesses considerable skill and resources.
