## Overview

[[Wormcrawl]] is an island situated off the coast of [[Chult]]. It serves as a site of dark magical significance, housing the reanimated followers of a long-dead cult that was restored to unholy life by the necromancer [[Kyusse]]. The location remains relatively obscure, deliberately shrouded in secrecy by the [[House of Knowledge]], which actively suppresses information about [[Wormcrawl]] from reaching wizards and magical scholars.
