## Overview

An illegal drinking establishment concealed in the basement of a residential building in [[Neverwinter]]. The speakeasy operates under the radar, serving various spirits and homebrewed liqueurs to patrons seeking refuge from the law or simply a discreet place to drink. The establishment maintains a facade of normalcy as a storage area or cellar, though those in the know can find their way to the bar.

## Description

The speakeasy features a modest bar setup stocked with an assortment of spirits and homebrewed liqueurs of varying quality and origin. The basement setting gives it a cramped, intimate atmosphere typical of underground establishments. Behind the main alcohol table sits a hidden metal door, its surface inscribed with necromantic runes—a clear indication that something far more sinister than alcohol production occurs in the adjoining space.

## Key Events

The discovery of the speakeasy's true purpose marked a significant turning point in the campaign. Investigation revealed that the establishment serves as a cover for far darker operations. Behind the metal door lies a laboratory dedicated to grotesque biological experimentation: a worm hatchery and the production of tainted healing potions. This revelation suggests the speakeasy is merely the visible front of a much larger conspiracy involving corruption of healing magic and possible undead creation in [[Neverwinter]].
